# Spain (ES) — Country Media Prompt Pack

Currency: `EUR`

Read [Country Media Production System](../00_COUNTRY_MEDIA_SYSTEM.md) before using these prompts. Every generation is assembled as:

`Global Premium Image Contract + role contract + country prompt body below`.

## Visual thesis

Warm urban modernity: Madrid or Barcelona should feel social, architectural and contemporary, with sun-warmed materials and everyday café/market life kept elegant.

### Preferred local cues

- Madrid/Barcelona stone, tile, balconies and contemporary streets
- coffee, pastry, simple tapas or market context
- warm limestone, terracotta in restraint, dark timber and metal
- strong but soft-edged Mediterranean daylight
- modern card/contactless retail context

### Avoid

- flamenco, bullfighting or sangria clichés
- Gaudí landmarks as the only Barcelona shorthand
- red-yellow flag palette
- overly rustic village aesthetic for the whole country

## P01 — Hero Wide

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/es/hero/es-hero-v01.webp`

Photograph an elegant Madrid or Barcelona urban scene in late-afternoon light: warm stone façades, balconies or contemporary architecture, shaded pavement, subtle café/retail life and naturally moving locals. Keep colors sophisticated—sand, charcoal, deep green, muted terracotta—and reserve one calm side for interface overlay.

Composition: one coherent contemporary scene, not a collage. Keep the main visual interest away from at least one outer third so product UI can coexist with the crop. Preserve architectural lines and realistic scale. People, if present, should feel incidental and unposed.

## P02 — Everyday Value

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/es/everyday-value/es-everyday-value-v01.webp`

Show a believable everyday Spanish café or casual food moment: café con leche/espresso, pastry or a small tapas-style plate, simple ceramic/glassware and warm stone/timber surface. Keep it ordinary and refined, not a tourism spread or feast.

Frame at table/counter/hand level rather than as a product advertisement. Do not print an invented price as a focal element. Any signage should be incidental and plausible, not AI-generated gibberish dominating the scene.

## P03 — Payment Culture

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/es/payment-culture/es-payment-culture-v01.webp`

Show a natural card/contactless payment at a contemporary Spanish café, bakery or market stall with stable terminal geometry and subtle local material cues. The interaction should feel routine, not staged.

Show the transaction naturally inside a real-feeling setting. Keep hands anatomically correct, device geometry plausible and the payment terminal/phone secondary to the human context. No visible bank/app logos unless they are incidental and accurate; preferably use neutral interfaces.

## P04 — Local Detail

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/es/local-detail/es-local-detail-v01.webp`

Create a restrained urban Spain detail: patterned tile used sparingly, ceramic cup, stone/timber surface, receipt edge with no readable totals and warm street light beyond. Avoid souvenir tiles or decorative cliché.

Treat this as a premium editorial insert: tactile materials, quiet composition, a small number of meaningful local cues and excellent light. No souvenir arrangement, flag palette, postcard montage or overloaded prop styling.

## P05 — Desktop Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/es/desktop/es-interface-desktop-v01.webp`

Create a 16:10 premium desktop design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on Spain. Use the sample conversion **100 GBP → EUR**. Use warm sand/ivory, charcoal, deep forest and muted terracotta/brass accents. Make the EUR result dominant and pair it with one sun-warmed city photograph. The screen should communicate: source amount → destination amount → local context. Make the numeric result the largest element. Use only minimal readable text: “Cultural Currency”, country name, currency codes, amount and result. Integrate the P01 visual language as one restrained destination image area. Do not turn the interface into a travel booking site or analytics dashboard.

## P06 — Mobile Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/es/mobile/es-interface-mobile-v01.webp`

Create a 9:16 premium mobile design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on Spain, using **100 GBP → EUR**. Translate the desktop hierarchy into a believable single-column mobile flow: compact source/destination selector, large conversion result, one editorial country image, then concise local-value/payment context. Keep the mobile layout airy and warm; one strong result, one small destination photo and minimal contextual copy. Keep tap targets believable, spacing generous and visible copy minimal. No tiny dashboard cards, fake phone chrome, floating glass panels or crowded widgets.
