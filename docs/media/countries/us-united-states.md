# United States (US) — Country Media Prompt Pack

Currency: `USD`

Read [Country Media Production System](../00_COUNTRY_MEDIA_SYSTEM.md) before using these prompts. Every generation is assembled as:

`Global Premium Image Contract + role contract + country prompt body below`.

## Visual thesis

Cosmopolitan scale and confident utility: represent the United States through contemporary urban life, material detail and everyday commerce rather than patriotic symbols or generic corporate stock.

### Preferred local cues

- New York, Chicago or another major-city street grid and mixed architecture
- coffee shop, diner, bodega or contemporary casual retail used with restraint
- steel, brick, glass, subway/street texture and strong directional light
- diverse ordinary city population shown naturally
- tap/card payment in realistic contemporary retail

### Avoid

- oversized US flags, eagles or patriotic montage
- Times Square as the default image
- Wall Street/finance clichés
- staged smiling-corporate stock photography

## P01 — Hero Wide

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/us/hero/us-hero-v01.webp`

Photograph a sophisticated New York or Chicago street in late-afternoon light from human height: strong architecture, crosswalk/street rhythm, subtle transit or storefront detail and a diverse handful of pedestrians who are not posing. Favor warm stone, steel, brick and deep shadow over saturated signage. Keep the composition editorial and leave one calmer third for UI.

Composition: one coherent contemporary scene, not a collage. Keep the main visual interest away from at least one outer third so product UI can coexist with the crop. Preserve architectural lines and realistic scale. People, if present, should feel incidental and unposed.

## P02 — Everyday Value

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/us/everyday-value/us-everyday-value-v01.webp`

Show a refined but ordinary US city spending moment: specialty coffee and a simple pastry, counter lunch, or small deli/café purchase in a real-feeling urban environment. Include tactile paper cup/ceramic, tray, countertop and subtle street depth. Avoid chain branding, oversized food styling or visible invented prices.

Frame at table/counter/hand level rather than as a product advertisement. Do not print an invented price as a focal element. Any signage should be incidental and plausible, not AI-generated gibberish dominating the scene.

## P03 — Payment Culture

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/us/payment-culture/us-payment-culture-v01.webp`

Show a natural tap-to-pay card or phone interaction at a modern café, bakery or neighborhood shop in a US city. The terminal should look contemporary but generic; include an understated tip-screen shape only if no readable fabricated values are shown. Keep the human moment observational, not advertising-like.

Show the transaction naturally inside a real-feeling setting. Keep hands anatomically correct, device geometry plausible and the payment terminal/phone secondary to the human context. No visible bank/app logos unless they are incidental and accurate; preferably use neutral interfaces.

## P04 — Local Detail

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/us/local-detail/us-local-detail-v01.webp`

Create an urban American detail image with two or three cues: brushed metal counter, takeaway cup or ceramic, subway/grid texture, receipt edge, brick/glass reflections and a glimpse of city movement. Make it cinematic and materially rich without turning into Americana props.

Treat this as a premium editorial insert: tactile materials, quiet composition, a small number of meaningful local cues and excellent light. No souvenir arrangement, flag palette, postcard montage or overloaded prop styling.

## P05 — Desktop Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/us/desktop/us-interface-desktop-v01.webp`

Create a 16:10 premium desktop design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on United States. Use the sample conversion **100 EUR → USD**. Use warm ivory, charcoal, deep forest and a restrained brass accent; make the USD result dominant and clean. The country image should feel like premium urban editorial photography, not a fintech stock photo. The screen should communicate: source amount → destination amount → local context. Make the numeric result the largest element. Use only minimal readable text: “Cultural Currency”, country name, currency codes, amount and result. Integrate the P01 visual language as one restrained destination image area. Do not turn the interface into a travel booking site or analytics dashboard.

## P06 — Mobile Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/us/mobile/us-interface-mobile-v01.webp`

Create a 9:16 premium mobile design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on United States, using **100 EUR → USD**. Translate the desktop hierarchy into a believable single-column mobile flow: compact source/destination selector, large conversion result, one editorial country image, then concise local-value/payment context. The mobile concept should feel fast and confident: large USD result, one compact city crop and concise local context with strong spacing, no badge-heavy fintech language. Keep tap targets believable, spacing generous and visible copy minimal. No tiny dashboard cards, fake phone chrome, floating glass panels or crowded widgets.
