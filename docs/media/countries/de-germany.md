# Germany (DE) — Country Media Prompt Pack

Currency: `EUR`

Read [Country Media Production System](../00_COUNTRY_MEDIA_SYSTEM.md) before using these prompts. Every generation is assembled as:

`Global Premium Image Contract + role contract + country prompt body below`.

## Visual thesis

Contemporary German clarity: precise architecture, public transport, café/market life and material honesty should feel efficient and cultured rather than cold or stereotypically industrial.

### Preferred local cues

- Berlin, Hamburg or Munich contemporary streets
- tram/U-Bahn/S-Bahn geometry used subtly
- concrete, stone, brick, glass and warm timber interiors
- bakery/café or market everyday context
- card payment shown without suggesting cash is absent

### Avoid

- Oktoberfest, lederhosen or beer-hall imagery as national default
- Brandenburg Gate as mandatory landmark
- autobahn/car clichés
- overly sterile corporate architecture

## P01 — Hero Wide

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/de/hero/de-hero-v01.webp`

Photograph a contemporary Berlin or Hamburg urban scene in crisp morning or late-afternoon light: strong architectural geometry, rail/tram/subway cues, cyclists/pedestrians and a few warm shop interiors. Use neutral concrete/brick/stone with deep green and warm window accents. Compose with disciplined lines and one quieter area for interface use.

Composition: one coherent contemporary scene, not a collage. Keep the main visual interest away from at least one outer third so product UI can coexist with the crop. Preserve architectural lines and realistic scale. People, if present, should feel incidental and unposed.

## P02 — Everyday Value

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/de/everyday-value/de-everyday-value-v01.webp`

Create an ordinary but beautifully observed German bakery or café purchase: coffee with a bread roll/pastry or simple lunch component, paper bag, ceramic cup and durable counter materials. Keep the scene practical, tactile and unforced; no fabricated price board.

Frame at table/counter/hand level rather than as a product advertisement. Do not print an invented price as a focal element. Any signage should be incidental and plausible, not AI-generated gibberish dominating the scene.

## P03 — Payment Culture

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/de/payment-culture/de-payment-culture-v01.webp`

Show a realistic card/contactless payment at a modern German bakery, café or food-market counter while allowing subtle cash-register context in the environment. Do not imply one universal payment habit. Keep device branding neutral and the composition calm.

Show the transaction naturally inside a real-feeling setting. Keep hands anatomically correct, device geometry plausible and the payment terminal/phone secondary to the human context. No visible bank/app logos unless they are incidental and accurate; preferably use neutral interfaces.

## P04 — Local Detail

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/de/local-detail/de-local-detail-v01.webp`

Build a restrained German urban detail around transit geometry, stone/concrete, bakery paper, ceramic cup and a glimpse of bicycle/rail infrastructure. Strong lines and material precision should carry the image, not stereotypes.

Treat this as a premium editorial insert: tactile materials, quiet composition, a small number of meaningful local cues and excellent light. No souvenir arrangement, flag palette, postcard montage or overloaded prop styling.

## P05 — Desktop Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/de/desktop/de-interface-desktop-v01.webp`

Create a 16:10 premium desktop design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on Germany. Use the sample conversion **100 USD → EUR**. Use limestone/ivory, charcoal, deep forest and muted warm metal. The EUR result should feel exact and trustworthy; use one disciplined Berlin/Hamburg photograph with architectural geometry. The screen should communicate: source amount → destination amount → local context. Make the numeric result the largest element. Use only minimal readable text: “Cultural Currency”, country name, currency codes, amount and result. Integrate the P01 visual language as one restrained destination image area. Do not turn the interface into a travel booking site or analytics dashboard.

## P06 — Mobile Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/de/mobile/de-interface-mobile-v01.webp`

Create a 9:16 premium mobile design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on Germany, using **100 USD → EUR**. Translate the desktop hierarchy into a believable single-column mobile flow: compact source/destination selector, large conversion result, one editorial country image, then concise local-value/payment context. Keep the mobile layout particularly structured and precise, with a large EUR result and one quiet urban photograph rather than multiple cards. Keep tap targets believable, spacing generous and visible copy minimal. No tiny dashboard cards, fake phone chrome, floating glass panels or crowded widgets.
