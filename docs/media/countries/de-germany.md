# Germany (DE) — Country Media Prompt Pack

Currency: `EUR`

Read [Country Media Production System](../00_COUNTRY_MEDIA_SYSTEM.md) before using these prompts. Every generation is assembled as:

`Global Premium Image Contract + role contract + country prompt body below`.

## Visual thesis

Contemporary German clarity: precise architecture, public transport, café/market life and material honesty should feel efficient and cultured rather than cold or stereotypically industrial.

### Premium material palette

- honest concrete and stone
- brick
- warm timber
- matte black metal

### Premium mood keywords

- disciplined clarity
- material honesty
- useful beauty
- lived-in precision

### Premium image goals

- premium first, authentic second, iconic only third;
- communicate the country through atmosphere, material, light, rhythm and everyday behaviour before relying on landmarks;
- make every image feel editorial and useful enough to sit inside an expensive real product, not a tourism campaign;
- preserve one clear visual story per frame and enough compositional calm for interface use where relevant.

### Preferred local cues

- Berlin, Hamburg or Munich contemporary streets
- tram/U-Bahn/S-Bahn geometry used subtly
- concrete, stone, brick, glass and warm timber interiors
- bakery/café or market everyday context
- card payment shown without suggesting cash is absent

### Avoid

- Oktoberfest, lederhosen or beer-hall imagery as national default
- Brandenburg Gate as mandatory landmark
- autobahn/car clichés
- overly sterile corporate architecture
