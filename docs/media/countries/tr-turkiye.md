# Türkiye (TR) — Country Media Prompt Pack

Currency: `TRY`

Read [Country Media Production System](../00_COUNTRY_MEDIA_SYSTEM.md) before using these prompts. Every generation is assembled as:

`Global Premium Image Contract + role contract + country prompt body below`.

## Visual thesis

Istanbul should feel layered, cosmopolitan and materially rich: Bosphorus light, ferries, contemporary streets, tea/café and retail life balanced without orientalist spectacle.

### Premium material palette

- stone and timber
- tea glass/ceramic
- deep teal metal/water reflections
- restrained copper

### Premium mood keywords

- layered cosmopolitan richness
- waterfront warmth
- tactile complexity
- controlled vitality

### Premium image goals

- premium first, authentic second, iconic only third;
- communicate the country through atmosphere, material, light, rhythm and everyday behaviour before relying on landmarks;
- make every image feel editorial and useful enough to sit inside an expensive real product, not a tourism campaign;
- preserve one clear visual story per frame and enough compositional calm for interface use where relevant.

### Preferred local cues

- Istanbul waterfront/ferry, layered hills or contemporary street life
- tea, simit, coffee or bakery/market details used naturally
- stone, timber, metal, glass and warm textile notes
- golden/blue-hour Bosphorus light
- card/contactless or phone payment in contemporary daily commerce

### Avoid

- mosque silhouettes as the only visual identity
- carpet/bazaar overload
- orientalist exoticism
- flag-red domination or staged traditional costume
