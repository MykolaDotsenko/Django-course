# Thailand (TH) — Country Media Prompt Pack

Currency: `THB`

Read [Country Media Production System](../00_COUNTRY_MEDIA_SYSTEM.md) before using these prompts. Every generation is assembled as:

`Global Premium Image Contract + role contract + country prompt body below`.

## Visual thesis

Bangkok should feel layered, warm and contemporary: street food, transit, tropical city texture and digital commerce represented with sophistication rather than tourism spectacle.

### Preferred local cues

- Bangkok streets, BTS/urban transport or mixed contemporary architecture
- street-food/café/market context handled cleanly
- warm metal, concrete, tile, tropical greenery and evening light
- QR/card/mobile payment used naturally
- humans in ordinary urban rhythm rather than staged travel imagery

### Avoid

- temple + tuk-tuk + elephant collage
- hyper-saturated night-market neon
- spa/resort imagery as the national default
- orientalist exoticism

## P01 — Hero Wide

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/th/hero/th-hero-v01.webp`

Photograph Bangkok at late afternoon or early evening from pedestrian height: layered contemporary street depth, BTS/transport or market/café cues, humid warm light, tropical greenery and naturally moving locals. Use warm stone, deep green, charcoal and restrained amber/red highlights. Keep one calmer third for UI.

Composition: one coherent contemporary scene, not a collage. Keep the main visual interest away from at least one outer third so product UI can coexist with the crop. Preserve architectural lines and realistic scale. People, if present, should feel incidental and unposed.

## P02 — Everyday Value

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/th/everyday-value/th-everyday-value-v01.webp`

Show a premium editorial everyday Bangkok purchase: a simple street-food dish, café drink or small meal served in a real contemporary setting. Preserve stainless steel, ceramic/plastic, table texture and human context without glamorizing or sanitizing the scene into a luxury restaurant ad.

Frame at table/counter/hand level rather than as a product advertisement. Do not print an invented price as a focal element. Any signage should be incidental and plausible, not AI-generated gibberish dominating the scene.

## P03 — Payment Culture

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/th/payment-culture/th-payment-culture-v01.webp`

Show a natural QR or card/phone payment at a Bangkok café, food stall or small modern retailer. The code/device should look plausible but generic, with no financial-app branding; keep the transaction integrated into the wider local scene.

Show the transaction naturally inside a real-feeling setting. Keep hands anatomically correct, device geometry plausible and the payment terminal/phone secondary to the human context. No visible bank/app logos unless they are incidental and accurate; preferably use neutral interfaces.

## P04 — Local Detail

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/th/local-detail/th-local-detail-v01.webp`

Create a tactile Bangkok detail with stainless food-counter texture, cup/bowl, transit or street reflection, tropical leaf/shade and warm evening light. Keep the frame selective and elegant, not cluttered.

Treat this as a premium editorial insert: tactile materials, quiet composition, a small number of meaningful local cues and excellent light. No souvenir arrangement, flag palette, postcard montage or overloaded prop styling.

## P05 — Desktop Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/th/desktop/th-interface-desktop-v01.webp`

Create a 16:10 premium desktop design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on Thailand. Use the sample conversion **100 EUR → THB**. Use warm ivory, charcoal, deep tropical green and muted amber/copper. THB should dominate typographically, with one atmospheric Bangkok image carrying local identity. The screen should communicate: source amount → destination amount → local context. Make the numeric result the largest element. Use only minimal readable text: “Cultural Currency”, country name, currency codes, amount and result. Integrate the P01 visual language as one restrained destination image area. Do not turn the interface into a travel booking site or analytics dashboard.

## P06 — Mobile Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/th/mobile/th-interface-mobile-v01.webp`

Create a 9:16 premium mobile design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on Thailand, using **100 EUR → THB**. Translate the desktop hierarchy into a believable single-column mobile flow: compact source/destination selector, large conversion result, one editorial country image, then concise local-value/payment context. Keep the mobile concept warm and energetic but controlled: one Bangkok crop, large THB result and concise context, no neon-card dashboard. Keep tap targets believable, spacing generous and visible copy minimal. No tiny dashboard cards, fake phone chrome, floating glass panels or crowded widgets.
