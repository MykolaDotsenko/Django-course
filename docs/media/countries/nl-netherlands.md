# Netherlands (NL) — Country Media Prompt Pack

Currency: `EUR`

Read [Country Media Production System](../00_COUNTRY_MEDIA_SYSTEM.md) before using these prompts. Every generation is assembled as:

`Global Premium Image Contract + role contract + country prompt body below`.

## Visual thesis

Dutch premium should feel intelligent, urban and functional: Amsterdam or Rotterdam architecture, water, cycling and modern retail expressed with clean composition rather than postcard canal nostalgia.

### Preferred local cues

- Amsterdam canal architecture or Rotterdam modern geometry
- bicycles as incidental city infrastructure, not staged props
- café/bakery or simple lunch context
- brick, glass, water reflections, dark metal and light timber
- digital/card payment context

### Avoid

- tulips, windmills and wooden shoes as default national shorthand
- orange flag/festival palette
- bicycle posed as a tourism prop
- canal postcard with no everyday life

## P01 — Hero Wide

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/nl/hero/nl-hero-v01.webp`

Photograph Amsterdam or Rotterdam from pedestrian height in crisp soft daylight: strong brick or contemporary architecture, water or tram/cycle infrastructure used naturally, a few commuters and restrained reflections. Use brick red only as a minor material note against charcoal, stone, deep green and water tones. Reserve a calmer side for UI.

Composition: one coherent contemporary scene, not a collage. Keep the main visual interest away from at least one outer third so product UI can coexist with the crop. Preserve architectural lines and realistic scale. People, if present, should feel incidental and unposed.

## P02 — Everyday Value

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/nl/everyday-value/nl-everyday-value-v01.webp`

Show a refined but ordinary Dutch café/bakery or lunch purchase: coffee, pastry/sandwich and simple table/counter materials, with a bicycle/street/canal hint out of focus if natural. The scene should feel efficient and contemporary rather than rustic.

Frame at table/counter/hand level rather than as a product advertisement. Do not print an invented price as a focal element. Any signage should be incidental and plausible, not AI-generated gibberish dominating the scene.

## P03 — Payment Culture

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/nl/payment-culture/nl-payment-culture-v01.webp`

Show a natural card/contactless payment at a modern Dutch café, bakery or grocery-style counter. Keep the terminal generic, environment clean and urban, and any bicycle/canal cue subtle in the background.

Show the transaction naturally inside a real-feeling setting. Keep hands anatomically correct, device geometry plausible and the payment terminal/phone secondary to the human context. No visible bank/app logos unless they are incidental and accurate; preferably use neutral interfaces.

## P04 — Local Detail

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/nl/local-detail/nl-local-detail-v01.webp`

Create a sophisticated Dutch city detail with brick, canal reflection or glass, bicycle-frame geometry, ceramic cup and folded paper. Use only a few elements and strong clean lines; no tulip/windmill motifs.

Treat this as a premium editorial insert: tactile materials, quiet composition, a small number of meaningful local cues and excellent light. No souvenir arrangement, flag palette, postcard montage or overloaded prop styling.

## P05 — Desktop Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/nl/desktop/nl-interface-desktop-v01.webp`

Create a 16:10 premium desktop design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on Netherlands. Use the sample conversion **100 USD → EUR**. Use warm ivory, charcoal, deep forest and muted brick/copper accents. Keep the EUR result large and simple; pair it with one clean Amsterdam/Rotterdam photograph. The screen should communicate: source amount → destination amount → local context. Make the numeric result the largest element. Use only minimal readable text: “Cultural Currency”, country name, currency codes, amount and result. Integrate the P01 visual language as one restrained destination image area. Do not turn the interface into a travel booking site or analytics dashboard.

## P06 — Mobile Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/nl/mobile/nl-interface-mobile-v01.webp`

Create a 9:16 premium mobile design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on Netherlands, using **100 USD → EUR**. Translate the desktop hierarchy into a believable single-column mobile flow: compact source/destination selector, large conversion result, one editorial country image, then concise local-value/payment context. Make the mobile concept crisp and functional with generous whitespace, large EUR numerals and one architectural/canal crop. Keep tap targets believable, spacing generous and visible copy minimal. No tiny dashboard cards, fake phone chrome, floating glass panels or crowded widgets.
