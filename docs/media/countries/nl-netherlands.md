# Netherlands (NL) — Country Media Prompt Pack

Currency: `EUR`

Read [Country Media Production System](../00_COUNTRY_MEDIA_SYSTEM.md) before using these prompts. Every generation is assembled as:

`Global Premium Image Contract + role contract + country prompt body below`.

## Visual thesis

Dutch premium should feel intelligent, urban and functional: Amsterdam or Rotterdam architecture, water, cycling and modern retail expressed with clean composition rather than postcard canal nostalgia.

### Premium material palette

- brick
- canal/glass reflections
- black metal
- light timber

### Premium mood keywords

- intelligent urban calm
- clean functionality
- design awareness
- efficient beauty

### Premium image goals

- premium first, authentic second, iconic only third;
- communicate the country through atmosphere, material, light, rhythm and everyday behaviour before relying on landmarks;
- make every image feel editorial and useful enough to sit inside an expensive real product, not a tourism campaign;
- preserve one clear visual story per frame and enough compositional calm for interface use where relevant.

### Preferred local cues

- Amsterdam canal architecture or Rotterdam modern geometry
- bicycles as incidental city infrastructure, not staged props
- café/bakery or simple lunch context
- brick, glass, water reflections, dark metal and light timber
- digital/card payment context

### Avoid

- tulips, windmills and wooden shoes as default national shorthand
- orange flag/festival palette
- bicycle posed as a tourism prop
- canal postcard with no everyday life

## P03 — Payment Culture

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/nl/payment-culture/nl-payment-culture-v01.webp`

**Premium execution:** The payment gesture should occupy only about 20–30% of the visual story; the environment should communicate the country. Use a natural 40–60 mm documentary perspective, anatomically correct hands, plausible device proportions and brick / canal/glass reflections as environmental anchors. Make the moment routine, discreet and trustworthy rather than fintech advertising.

Show a natural card/contactless payment at a modern Dutch café, bakery or grocery-style counter. Keep the terminal generic, environment clean and urban, and any bicycle/canal cue subtle in the background.

Show the transaction naturally inside a real-feeling setting. Keep hands anatomically correct, device geometry plausible and the payment terminal/phone secondary to the human context. No visible bank/app logos unless they are incidental and accurate; preferably use neutral interfaces.
