# Switzerland (CH) — Country Media Prompt Pack

Currency: `CHF`

Read [Country Media Production System](../00_COUNTRY_MEDIA_SYSTEM.md) before using these prompts. Every generation is assembled as:

`Global Premium Image Contract + role contract + country prompt body below`.

## Visual thesis

Swiss premium should feel materially precise, calm and discreet: Zurich or Geneva urban life, transport, lakeside light and high-quality everyday environments without turning into watch/bank/luxury clichés.

### Preferred local cues

- Zurich or Geneva stone/glass urban architecture
- tram or rail infrastructure used subtly
- lake/river light and mountain presence only when naturally contextual
- clean café/bakery materials, brushed metal, stone and timber
- contactless/card payment in a refined everyday setting

### Avoid

- Swiss flag domination
- watches, private banking, gold bars or luxury-car shorthand
- alpine chalet imagery as the only Switzerland
- perfectly sterile scenes with no human life

## P01 — Hero Wide

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/ch/hero/ch-hero-v01.webp`

Photograph Zurich or Geneva in crisp soft daylight or blue hour: elegant stone/glass architecture, a subtle tram or pedestrian rhythm, a glimpse of lake/river light if compositionally natural, and restrained human activity. Use cool stone, warm interior light, deep forest and charcoal, with a calm outer third for UI. The feeling should be exact, discreet and expensive rather than flashy.

Composition: one coherent contemporary scene, not a collage. Keep the main visual interest away from at least one outer third so product UI can coexist with the crop. Preserve architectural lines and realistic scale. People, if present, should feel incidental and unposed.

## P02 — Everyday Value

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/ch/everyday-value/ch-everyday-value-v01.webp`

Show a believable Swiss café or bakery purchase: coffee, pastry or simple lunch element on stone/timber, natural paper packaging and high-quality everyday materials. Keep the image ordinary but impeccably observed; no watches, luxury logos or invented pricing.

Frame at table/counter/hand level rather than as a product advertisement. Do not print an invented price as a focal element. Any signage should be incidental and plausible, not AI-generated gibberish dominating the scene.

## P03 — Payment Culture

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/ch/payment-culture/ch-payment-culture-v01.webp`

Show a natural contactless card/phone payment at a contemporary Swiss café, bakery or food hall. Terminal/device should be understated, with refined materials and a hint of tram/street/lake context beyond the counter.

Show the transaction naturally inside a real-feeling setting. Keep hands anatomically correct, device geometry plausible and the payment terminal/phone secondary to the human context. No visible bank/app logos unless they are incidental and accurate; preferably use neutral interfaces.

## P04 — Local Detail

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/ch/local-detail/ch-local-detail-v01.webp`

Create a quiet Swiss detail image with brushed metal, pale stone, tram/rail geometry, ceramic cup and soft lake/city reflection. Use precise lines and restrained luxury; avoid obvious flag, watch or mountain souvenir motifs.

Treat this as a premium editorial insert: tactile materials, quiet composition, a small number of meaningful local cues and excellent light. No souvenir arrangement, flag palette, postcard montage or overloaded prop styling.

## P05 — Desktop Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/ch/desktop/ch-interface-desktop-v01.webp`

Create a 16:10 premium desktop design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on Switzerland. Use the sample conversion **100 EUR → CHF**. Use warm ivory, charcoal, deep forest and a cool stone/bronze accent. Make the CHF result feel exact and understated; a single Zurich/Geneva image should communicate refinement without luxury-brand clichés. The screen should communicate: source amount → destination amount → local context. Make the numeric result the largest element. Use only minimal readable text: “Cultural Currency”, country name, currency codes, amount and result. Integrate the P01 visual language as one restrained destination image area. Do not turn the interface into a travel booking site or analytics dashboard.

## P06 — Mobile Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/ch/mobile/ch-interface-mobile-v01.webp`

Create a 9:16 premium mobile design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on Switzerland, using **100 EUR → CHF**. Translate the desktop hierarchy into a believable single-column mobile flow: compact source/destination selector, large conversion result, one editorial country image, then concise local-value/payment context. Keep the mobile surface extremely disciplined: large CHF numerals, generous whitespace, one crisp city crop and minimal metadata. Keep tap targets believable, spacing generous and visible copy minimal. No tiny dashboard cards, fake phone chrome, floating glass panels or crowded widgets.
