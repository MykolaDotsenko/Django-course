# Brazil (BR) — Country Media Prompt Pack

Currency: `BRL`

Read [Country Media Production System](../00_COUNTRY_MEDIA_SYSTEM.md) before using these prompts. Every generation is assembled as:

`Global Premium Image Contract + role contract + country prompt body below`.

## Visual thesis

Brazil should feel contemporary, energetic and sophisticated through São Paulo urban life, café/food culture and digital commerce, without beach, carnival or tropical cliché dominating the product.

### Preferred local cues

- São Paulo streets, modernist/high-rise urban fabric and mixed-use neighbourhoods
- coffee, bakery/snack or casual meal context
- concrete, tile, timber, greenery and warm humid light
- card/phone/QR payment in everyday settings
- diverse ordinary urban population shown naturally

### Avoid

- Carnival, football or Copacabana as default representation
- Brazil-flag green/yellow palette
- jungle imagery disconnected from city life
- hyper-saturated tropical advertising aesthetic

## P01 — Hero Wide

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/br/hero/br-hero-v01.webp`

Photograph a sophisticated São Paulo urban scene in soft late-afternoon light: modernist/high-rise layers, street-level café/retail, greenery and unposed residents. Use concrete grey, deep green, warm timber and restrained terracotta/amber rather than flag colors. Leave a calm outer third for UI.

Composition: one coherent contemporary scene, not a collage. Keep the main visual interest away from at least one outer third so product UI can coexist with the crop. Preserve architectural lines and realistic scale. People, if present, should feel incidental and unposed.

## P02 — Everyday Value

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/br/everyday-value/br-everyday-value-v01.webp`

Show a believable everyday Brazilian city purchase: coffee and pão de queijo/bakery item, simple lunch or casual café moment with tactile ceramic/paper/stone materials. Keep the scene relaxed and real, not a food commercial.

Frame at table/counter/hand level rather than as a product advertisement. Do not print an invented price as a focal element. Any signage should be incidental and plausible, not AI-generated gibberish dominating the scene.

## P03 — Payment Culture

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/br/payment-culture/br-payment-culture-v01.webp`

Show a natural card/phone or generic QR/Pix-like payment interaction at a contemporary São Paulo café, bakery or small retailer. Do not display real financial-app logos or fabricated account information.

Show the transaction naturally inside a real-feeling setting. Keep hands anatomically correct, device geometry plausible and the payment terminal/phone secondary to the human context. No visible bank/app logos unless they are incidental and accurate; preferably use neutral interfaces.

## P04 — Local Detail

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/br/local-detail/br-local-detail-v01.webp`

Create an editorial São Paulo detail with concrete/tile, coffee cup, paper receipt edge, greenery shadow and layered street reflection. Keep it urban and materially rich, with no football/carnival props.

Treat this as a premium editorial insert: tactile materials, quiet composition, a small number of meaningful local cues and excellent light. No souvenir arrangement, flag palette, postcard montage or overloaded prop styling.

## P05 — Desktop Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/br/desktop/br-interface-desktop-v01.webp`

Create a 16:10 premium desktop design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on Brazil. Use the sample conversion **100 EUR → BRL**. Use warm ivory, charcoal, deep forest and restrained terracotta/copper. Make BRL numerals dominant and use one sophisticated São Paulo photograph to convey place. The screen should communicate: source amount → destination amount → local context. Make the numeric result the largest element. Use only minimal readable text: “Cultural Currency”, country name, currency codes, amount and result. Integrate the P01 visual language as one restrained destination image area. Do not turn the interface into a travel booking site or analytics dashboard.

## P06 — Mobile Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/br/mobile/br-interface-mobile-v01.webp`

Create a 9:16 premium mobile design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on Brazil, using **100 EUR → BRL**. Translate the desktop hierarchy into a believable single-column mobile flow: compact source/destination selector, large conversion result, one editorial country image, then concise local-value/payment context. Use one dense-but-controlled urban crop with strong negative space and a very large BRL result; keep secondary UI minimal. Keep tap targets believable, spacing generous and visible copy minimal. No tiny dashboard cards, fake phone chrome, floating glass panels or crowded widgets.
