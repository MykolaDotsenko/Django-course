# China (CN) — Country Media Prompt Pack

Currency: `CNY`

Read [Country Media Production System](../00_COUNTRY_MEDIA_SYSTEM.md) before using these prompts. Every generation is assembled as:

`Global Premium Image Contract + role contract + country prompt body below`.

## Visual thesis

Contemporary China should feel metropolitan, dense and technologically mature: Shanghai-style urban depth, transit and everyday commerce without futuristic spectacle or generic red-lantern clichés.

### Premium material palette

- stone and glass
- brushed metal
- warm timber/paper
- restrained city reflections

### Premium mood keywords

- metropolitan depth
- technological maturity
- controlled density
- quiet modern power

### Premium image goals

- premium first, authentic second, iconic only third;
- communicate the country through atmosphere, material, light, rhythm and everyday behaviour before relying on landmarks;
- make every image feel editorial and useful enough to sit inside an expensive real product, not a tourism campaign;
- preserve one clear visual story per frame and enough compositional calm for interface use where relevant.

### Preferred local cues

- Shanghai contemporary streets, metro entrances, mixed old/new urban fabric
- small café, casual food or modern retail context
- stone, glass, metal, warm wood and controlled signage
- evening reflections with restrained red accents
- QR/mobile payment shown generically and naturally

### Avoid

- Great Wall or Forbidden City as mandatory hero
- red lantern/dragon collage
- cyberpunk megacity exaggeration
- fake Chinese characters as a decorative focal point

## P03 — Payment Culture

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/cn/payment-culture/cn-payment-culture-v01.webp`

**Premium execution:** The payment gesture should occupy only about 20–30% of the visual story; the environment should communicate the country. Use a natural 40–60 mm documentary perspective, anatomically correct hands, plausible device proportions and stone and glass / brushed metal as environmental anchors. Make the moment routine, discreet and trustworthy rather than fintech advertising.

Show a generic mobile/QR payment interaction at a contemporary café or small retailer in Shanghai or another major city. The phone/QR should be plausible but carry no recognizable payment-app branding or readable fabricated account details. Keep it a human everyday moment, not a tech demo.

Show the transaction naturally inside a real-feeling setting. Keep hands anatomically correct, device geometry plausible and the payment terminal/phone secondary to the human context. No visible bank/app logos unless they are incidental and accurate; preferably use neutral interfaces.
