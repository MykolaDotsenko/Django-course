Currency: `EUR`

Read [Country Media Production System](../00_COUNTRY_MEDIA_SYSTEM.md) before using these prompts. Every generation is assembled as:

## Visual thesis

Italian sophistication through real urban life, patina, material richness and confident simplicity—more contemporary editorial Italy than postcard nostalgia.

### Premium material palette

- travertine and marble
- warm plaster
- stainless espresso-bar surfaces
- olive-toned timber/metal

### Premium mood keywords

- warm sophistication
- relaxed confidence
- urban sensuality
- lived-in elegance

### Premium image goals

- premium first, authentic second, iconic only third;
- communicate the country through atmosphere, material, light, rhythm and everyday behaviour before relying on landmarks;
- make every image feel editorial and useful enough to sit inside an expensive real product, not a tourism campaign;
- preserve one clear visual story per frame and enough compositional calm for interface use where relevant.

### Preferred local cues

- Milan or Rome streets, stone façades, arcades, scooters as incidental motion
- espresso bar, bakery or simple lunch context
- travertine, marble, wood, stainless steel and warm plaster
- late-afternoon Mediterranean light
- card/contactless moment in modern everyday retail

### Avoid

- Vespa + Colosseum + pizza cliché montage
- flag-color art direction
- luxury-fashion logos as the source of premium
- over-romanticized empty cobblestone postcard streets
