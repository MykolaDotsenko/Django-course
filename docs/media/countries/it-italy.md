# Italy (IT) — Country Media Prompt Pack

Currency: `EUR`

Read [Country Media Production System](../00_COUNTRY_MEDIA_SYSTEM.md) before using these prompts. Every generation is assembled as:

`Global Premium Image Contract + role contract + country prompt body below`.

## Visual thesis

Italian sophistication through real urban life, patina, material richness and confident simplicity—more contemporary editorial Italy than postcard nostalgia.

### Preferred local cues

- Milan or Rome streets, stone façades, arcades, scooters as incidental motion
- espresso bar, bakery or simple lunch context
- travertine, marble, wood, stainless steel and warm plaster
- late-afternoon Mediterranean light
- card/contactless moment in modern everyday retail

### Avoid

- Vespa + Colosseum + pizza cliché montage
- flag-color art direction
- luxury-fashion logos as the source of premium
- over-romanticized empty cobblestone postcard streets

## P01 — Hero Wide

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/it/hero/it-hero-v01.webp`

Photograph an elegant Milan or Rome street at warm late afternoon: refined façades, stone pavement, a small amount of scooter or pedestrian movement, café/retail depth and natural urban patina. Use warm stone, olive/deep green, charcoal and subtle brass tones. Keep the scene contemporary and lived-in, with a calm edge for UI.

Composition: one coherent contemporary scene, not a collage. Keep the main visual interest away from at least one outer third so product UI can coexist with the crop. Preserve architectural lines and realistic scale. People, if present, should feel incidental and unposed.

## P02 — Everyday Value

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/it/everyday-value/it-everyday-value-v01.webp`

Show an authentic everyday Italian espresso-bar or casual lunch moment: espresso in a small ceramic cup with cornetto/pastry or a simple panino, stainless/stone counter and natural human presence. It should feel fast, ordinary and beautifully designed by circumstance, not a styled luxury shoot.

Frame at table/counter/hand level rather than as a product advertisement. Do not print an invented price as a focal element. Any signage should be incidental and plausible, not AI-generated gibberish dominating the scene.

## P03 — Payment Culture

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/it/payment-culture/it-payment-culture-v01.webp`

Show a subtle card/contactless payment at an Italian café, bakery or small contemporary retail counter. Preserve tactile local materials and human warmth while keeping the device secondary and neutral.

Show the transaction naturally inside a real-feeling setting. Keep hands anatomically correct, device geometry plausible and the payment terminal/phone secondary to the human context. No visible bank/app logos unless they are incidental and accurate; preferably use neutral interfaces.

## P04 — Local Detail

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/it/local-detail/it-local-detail-v01.webp`

Create a tactile Italian urban detail using travertine or marble, stainless espresso-bar edge, ceramic cup, folded paper, warm plaster and a softly blurred street/scooter cue. Avoid souvenir objects and overt flag colors.

Treat this as a premium editorial insert: tactile materials, quiet composition, a small number of meaningful local cues and excellent light. No souvenir arrangement, flag palette, postcard montage or overloaded prop styling.

## P05 — Desktop Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/it/desktop/it-interface-desktop-v01.webp`

Create a 16:10 premium desktop design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on Italy. Use the sample conversion **100 USD → EUR**. Use warm ivory/travertine, charcoal, deep olive/forest and restrained bronze. The EUR result is large and elegant; integrate a single Milan/Rome photograph with warm material depth. The screen should communicate: source amount → destination amount → local context. Make the numeric result the largest element. Use only minimal readable text: “Cultural Currency”, country name, currency codes, amount and result. Integrate the P01 visual language as one restrained destination image area. Do not turn the interface into a travel booking site or analytics dashboard.

## P06 — Mobile Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/it/mobile/it-interface-mobile-v01.webp`

Create a 9:16 premium mobile design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on Italy, using **100 USD → EUR**. Translate the desktop hierarchy into a believable single-column mobile flow: compact source/destination selector, large conversion result, one editorial country image, then concise local-value/payment context. Let the mobile concept feel warm and refined, with an oversized EUR result and one tactile Italian image rather than decorative motifs. Keep tap targets believable, spacing generous and visible copy minimal. No tiny dashboard cards, fake phone chrome, floating glass panels or crowded widgets.
