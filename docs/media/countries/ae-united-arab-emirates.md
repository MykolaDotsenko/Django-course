# United Arab Emirates (AE) — Country Media Prompt Pack

Currency: `AED`

Read [Country Media Production System](../00_COUNTRY_MEDIA_SYSTEM.md) before using these prompts. Every generation is assembled as:

`Global Premium Image Contract + role contract + country prompt body below`.

## Visual thesis

The UAE should feel polished, contemporary and globally connected: Dubai or Abu Dhabi architecture, hospitality and everyday commerce rendered with restraint instead of gold-and-supercar spectacle.

### Preferred local cues

- Dubai/Abu Dhabi contemporary architecture and shaded public realm
- stone, glass, brushed metal, timber and refined hospitality materials
- café, mall-adjacent or urban daily-spending context
- clean desert light and warm evening illumination
- contactless/card/mobile payment in polished environments

### Avoid

- supercars, gold bars or private-jet clichés
- Burj Khalifa as obligatory full-frame hero
- excessive gold/black luxury palette
- desert-camp tourism as default country identity

## P01 — Hero Wide

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/ae/hero/ae-hero-v01.webp`

Photograph Dubai or Abu Dhabi in soft early-morning or late-afternoon light: refined contemporary architecture, shaded pedestrian space, subtle greenery and ordinary residents/visitors moving through a polished but believable city environment. Use limestone, warm sand, charcoal, deep green and restrained metallic notes. Reserve calm space for UI.

Composition: one coherent contemporary scene, not a collage. Keep the main visual interest away from at least one outer third so product UI can coexist with the crop. Preserve architectural lines and realistic scale. People, if present, should feel incidental and unposed.

## P02 — Everyday Value

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/ae/everyday-value/ae-everyday-value-v01.webp`

Show an ordinary but elegant UAE café or bakery spending moment: coffee/tea with a simple pastry or light meal in a high-quality urban setting. Keep materials refined without turning the scene into five-star-hotel luxury theatre.

Frame at table/counter/hand level rather than as a product advertisement. Do not print an invented price as a focal element. Any signage should be incidental and plausible, not AI-generated gibberish dominating the scene.

## P03 — Payment Culture

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/ae/payment-culture/ae-payment-culture-v01.webp`

Show a natural contactless card or phone payment in a Dubai/Abu Dhabi café, mall-adjacent bakery or urban retailer. Keep terminal branding neutral and the human interaction normal rather than aspirational advertising.

Show the transaction naturally inside a real-feeling setting. Keep hands anatomically correct, device geometry plausible and the payment terminal/phone secondary to the human context. No visible bank/app logos unless they are incidental and accurate; preferably use neutral interfaces.

## P04 — Local Detail

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/ae/local-detail/ae-local-detail-v01.webp`

Create a quiet UAE detail with limestone, brushed metal, coffee cup, subtle mashrabiya/shadow geometry or city reflection and warm desert-filtered light. Avoid gold ornament overload.

Treat this as a premium editorial insert: tactile materials, quiet composition, a small number of meaningful local cues and excellent light. No souvenir arrangement, flag palette, postcard montage or overloaded prop styling.

## P05 — Desktop Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/ae/desktop/ae-interface-desktop-v01.webp`

Create a 16:10 premium desktop design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on United Arab Emirates. Use the sample conversion **100 EUR → AED**. Use warm ivory/limestone, charcoal, deep forest and restrained sand/brass accents. Make AED numerals large and authoritative, with one refined contemporary-city image. The screen should communicate: source amount → destination amount → local context. Make the numeric result the largest element. Use only minimal readable text: “Cultural Currency”, country name, currency codes, amount and result. Integrate the P01 visual language as one restrained destination image area. Do not turn the interface into a travel booking site or analytics dashboard.

## P06 — Mobile Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/ae/mobile/ae-interface-mobile-v01.webp`

Create a 9:16 premium mobile design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on United Arab Emirates, using **100 EUR → AED**. Translate the desktop hierarchy into a believable single-column mobile flow: compact source/destination selector, large conversion result, one editorial country image, then concise local-value/payment context. Keep the mobile screen luxurious through spacing and material tone, not decoration: one UAE city crop, large AED result and minimal contextual rows. Keep tap targets believable, spacing generous and visible copy minimal. No tiny dashboard cards, fake phone chrome, floating glass panels or crowded widgets.
