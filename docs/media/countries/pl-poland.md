# Poland (PL) — Country Media Prompt Pack

Currency: `PLN`

Read [Country Media Production System](../00_COUNTRY_MEDIA_SYSTEM.md) before using these prompts. Every generation is assembled as:

`Global Premium Image Contract + role contract + country prompt body below`.

## Visual thesis

Contemporary Poland should feel dynamic and cultured: Warsaw modernity and Kraków material history can inform the language without turning into old-town tourism or Eastern-Europe stereotypes.

### Preferred local cues

- Warsaw contemporary skyline/street or Kraków stone urban texture
- tram infrastructure and active pedestrian streets
- café, bakery or casual lunch context
- brick, stone, plaster, dark metal and warm interiors
- card/contactless payment in modern daily life

### Avoid

- old-town square as mandatory postcard
- folk costume/pattern clichés
- grey post-Soviet stereotype
- flag-heavy red-white styling

## P01 — Hero Wide

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/pl/hero/pl-hero-v01.webp`

Photograph a refined Warsaw street or modern district, or a contemporary Kraków urban scene, in soft late-afternoon light: architecture with depth, tram/street rhythm, natural pedestrians and warm interiors. Use stone, brick, charcoal, deep green and muted amber; keep a calm area for UI.

Composition: one coherent contemporary scene, not a collage. Keep the main visual interest away from at least one outer third so product UI can coexist with the crop. Preserve architectural lines and realistic scale. People, if present, should feel incidental and unposed.

## P02 — Everyday Value

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/pl/everyday-value/pl-everyday-value-v01.webp`

Show a polished but everyday Polish café/bakery or lunch purchase: coffee, pastry/sandwich or simple meal detail on stone/timber with natural paper/ceramic. Keep it urban and believable, not folkloric or overly rustic.

Frame at table/counter/hand level rather than as a product advertisement. Do not print an invented price as a focal element. Any signage should be incidental and plausible, not AI-generated gibberish dominating the scene.

## P03 — Payment Culture

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/pl/payment-culture/pl-payment-culture-v01.webp`

Show a natural contactless card/phone payment at a Warsaw/Kraków café, bakery or small retail counter. Use contemporary environment cues and neutral device interfaces; avoid making a technology advertisement.

Show the transaction naturally inside a real-feeling setting. Keep hands anatomically correct, device geometry plausible and the payment terminal/phone secondary to the human context. No visible bank/app logos unless they are incidental and accurate; preferably use neutral interfaces.

## P04 — Local Detail

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/pl/local-detail/pl-local-detail-v01.webp`

Create a tactile Polish city detail with tram texture, stone/brick, ceramic cup, paper bag/receipt edge and warm window reflections. Keep it contemporary and editorial, not souvenir-like.

Treat this as a premium editorial insert: tactile materials, quiet composition, a small number of meaningful local cues and excellent light. No souvenir arrangement, flag palette, postcard montage or overloaded prop styling.

## P05 — Desktop Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/pl/desktop/pl-interface-desktop-v01.webp`

Create a 16:10 premium desktop design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on Poland. Use the sample conversion **100 EUR → PLN**. Use warm stone/ivory, charcoal, deep forest and muted amber/copper. Let PLN numerals dominate and use one strong Warsaw/Kraków image with contemporary depth. The screen should communicate: source amount → destination amount → local context. Make the numeric result the largest element. Use only minimal readable text: “Cultural Currency”, country name, currency codes, amount and result. Integrate the P01 visual language as one restrained destination image area. Do not turn the interface into a travel booking site or analytics dashboard.

## P06 — Mobile Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/pl/mobile/pl-interface-mobile-v01.webp`

Create a 9:16 premium mobile design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on Poland, using **100 EUR → PLN**. Translate the desktop hierarchy into a believable single-column mobile flow: compact source/destination selector, large conversion result, one editorial country image, then concise local-value/payment context. Make the mobile concept modern and confident: large PLN result, one city crop, clean controls and no decorative folk motifs. Keep tap targets believable, spacing generous and visible copy minimal. No tiny dashboard cards, fake phone chrome, floating glass panels or crowded widgets.
