## Visual thesis

Contemporary Poland should feel dynamic and cultured: Warsaw modernity and Kraków material history can inform the language without turning into old-town tourism or Eastern-Europe stereotypes.

### Premium material palette

- stone and brick
- dark metal
- warm plaster/timber
- tram-window reflections

### Premium mood keywords

- cultured contemporary Europe
- grounded energy
- urban confidence
- warm practicality

### Premium image goals

- premium first, authentic second, iconic only third;
- communicate the country through atmosphere, material, light, rhythm and everyday behaviour before relying on landmarks;
- make every image feel editorial and useful enough to sit inside an expensive real product, not a tourism campaign;
- preserve one clear visual story per frame and enough compositional calm for interface use where relevant.

### Preferred local cues

- Warsaw contemporary skyline/street or Kraków stone urban texture
- tram infrastructure and active pedestrian streets
- café, bakery or casual lunch context
- brick, stone, plaster, dark metal and warm interiors
- card/contactless payment in modern daily life

### Avoid

- old-town square as mandatory postcard
- folk costume/pattern clichés
- grey post-Soviet stereotype
- flag-heavy red-white styling
