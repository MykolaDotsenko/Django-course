# Czechia (CZ) — Country Media Prompt Pack

Currency: `CZK`

Read [Country Media Production System](../00_COUNTRY_MEDIA_SYSTEM.md) before using these prompts. Every generation is assembled as:

`Global Premium Image Contract + role contract + country prompt body below`.

## Visual thesis

Prague should feel architectural, tram-connected and lived-in, with patina and contemporary daily life balanced carefully so the product avoids medieval-tourism imagery.

### Preferred local cues

- Prague stone/plaster streets and tram infrastructure
- contemporary café/bakery context
- muted plaster, stone, dark timber and metal
- soft overcast or golden urban light
- card/contactless payment in modern venues

### Avoid

- Charles Bridge/castle as obligatory centerpiece
- beer mug imagery as national identity
- medieval/tour-guide styling
- souvenir marionettes or flag motifs

## P01 — Hero Wide

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/cz/hero/cz-hero-v01.webp`

Photograph a refined Prague street where historic plaster/stone architecture coexists with contemporary tram or pedestrian life. Use soft morning/evening light, restrained warm plaster, charcoal, deep green and muted brass. Avoid landmark-chasing; reserve one calm third for UI.

Composition: one coherent contemporary scene, not a collage. Keep the main visual interest away from at least one outer third so product UI can coexist with the crop. Preserve architectural lines and realistic scale. People, if present, should feel incidental and unposed.

## P02 — Everyday Value

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/cz/everyday-value/cz-everyday-value-v01.webp`

Create a modern Prague café or bakery spending moment with coffee, pastry/simple lunch and understated local materials. Keep the frame practical, elegant and contemporary, with no visible invented prices or beer-tourism styling.

Frame at table/counter/hand level rather than as a product advertisement. Do not print an invented price as a focal element. Any signage should be incidental and plausible, not AI-generated gibberish dominating the scene.

## P03 — Payment Culture

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/cz/payment-culture/cz-payment-culture-v01.webp`

Show a subtle card/contactless payment in a contemporary Prague café/bakery or design-led small shop. Let architecture/material cues identify place indirectly while keeping the transaction natural.

Show the transaction naturally inside a real-feeling setting. Keep hands anatomically correct, device geometry plausible and the payment terminal/phone secondary to the human context. No visible bank/app logos unless they are incidental and accurate; preferably use neutral interfaces.

## P04 — Local Detail

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/cz/local-detail/cz-local-detail-v01.webp`

Create a Prague detail study with tram geometry, aged plaster/stone, ceramic cup, dark timber and a folded receipt edge. The image should feel tactile and cultured without medieval or souvenir cues.

Treat this as a premium editorial insert: tactile materials, quiet composition, a small number of meaningful local cues and excellent light. No souvenir arrangement, flag palette, postcard montage or overloaded prop styling.

## P05 — Desktop Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/cz/desktop/cz-interface-desktop-v01.webp`

Create a 16:10 premium desktop design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on Czechia. Use the sample conversion **100 EUR → CZK**. Use warm plaster/ivory, charcoal, deep forest and muted bronze. Make CZK the typographic focal point, with one Prague image bringing material character. The screen should communicate: source amount → destination amount → local context. Make the numeric result the largest element. Use only minimal readable text: “Cultural Currency”, country name, currency codes, amount and result. Integrate the P01 visual language as one restrained destination image area. Do not turn the interface into a travel booking site or analytics dashboard.

## P06 — Mobile Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/cz/mobile/cz-interface-mobile-v01.webp`

Create a 9:16 premium mobile design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on Czechia, using **100 EUR → CZK**. Translate the desktop hierarchy into a believable single-column mobile flow: compact source/destination selector, large conversion result, one editorial country image, then concise local-value/payment context. Use a narrow architectural/tram crop with a very large CZK result and minimal secondary UI. Keep tap targets believable, spacing generous and visible copy minimal. No tiny dashboard cards, fake phone chrome, floating glass panels or crowded widgets.
