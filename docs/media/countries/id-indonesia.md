# Indonesia (ID) — Country Media Prompt Pack

Currency: `IDR`

Read [Country Media Production System](../00_COUNTRY_MEDIA_SYSTEM.md) before using these prompts. Every generation is assembled as:

`Global Premium Image Contract + role contract + country prompt body below`.

## Visual thesis

Indonesia should feel contemporary, tropical and urban through Jakarta everyday life, cafés, street/retail texture and digital payment rather than resort or Bali-only imagery.

### Premium material palette

- concrete and tile
- warm timber
- tropical foliage
- humid amber reflections

### Premium mood keywords

- tropical urban modernity
- soft density
- warm contemporary life
- grounded digital ease

### Premium image goals

- premium first, authentic second, iconic only third;
- communicate the country through atmosphere, material, light, rhythm and everyday behaviour before relying on landmarks;
- make every image feel editorial and useful enough to sit inside an expensive real product, not a tourism campaign;
- preserve one clear visual story per frame and enough compositional calm for interface use where relevant.

### Preferred local cues

- Jakarta streets, transit, modern mixed-use architecture and tropical greenery
- coffee, simple meal or everyday retail context
- concrete, timber, tile, metal and warm humid light
- QR/mobile/card payment in modern daily commerce
- layered but controlled tropical city atmosphere

### Avoid

- Bali resort/beach as the whole country
- rice terraces or temples as default hero
- tropical-vacation stock aesthetic
- overcrowded street scene without visual hierarchy

## P03 — Payment Culture

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/id/payment-culture/id-payment-culture-v01.webp`

**Premium execution:** The payment gesture should occupy only about 20–30% of the visual story; the environment should communicate the country. Use a natural 40–60 mm documentary perspective, anatomically correct hands, plausible device proportions and concrete and tile / warm timber as environmental anchors. Make the moment routine, discreet and trustworthy rather than fintech advertising.

Show a generic QR, phone or card payment at a contemporary Indonesian café, food counter or small urban retailer. The code/device should be plausible, brand-neutral and secondary to the real human context.

Show the transaction naturally inside a real-feeling setting. Keep hands anatomically correct, device geometry plausible and the payment terminal/phone secondary to the human context. No visible bank/app logos unless they are incidental and accurate; preferably use neutral interfaces.
