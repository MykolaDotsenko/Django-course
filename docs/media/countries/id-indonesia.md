# Indonesia (ID) — Country Media Prompt Pack

Currency: `IDR`

Read [Country Media Production System](../00_COUNTRY_MEDIA_SYSTEM.md) before using these prompts. Every generation is assembled as:

`Global Premium Image Contract + role contract + country prompt body below`.

## Visual thesis

Indonesia should feel contemporary, tropical and urban through Jakarta everyday life, cafés, street/retail texture and digital payment rather than resort or Bali-only imagery.

### Preferred local cues

- Jakarta streets, transit, modern mixed-use architecture and tropical greenery
- coffee, simple meal or everyday retail context
- concrete, timber, tile, metal and warm humid light
- QR/mobile/card payment in modern daily commerce
- layered but controlled tropical city atmosphere

### Avoid

- Bali resort/beach as the whole country
- rice terraces or temples as default hero
- tropical-vacation stock aesthetic
- overcrowded street scene without visual hierarchy

## P01 — Hero Wide

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/id/hero/id-hero-v01.webp`

Photograph contemporary Jakarta in warm late-afternoon or soft overcast tropical light: layered urban architecture, transit/sidewalk/retail cues, greenery and unposed residents. Use concrete, warm timber, deep tropical green, charcoal and restrained amber. Keep one side quieter for UI and avoid resort imagery.

Composition: one coherent contemporary scene, not a collage. Keep the main visual interest away from at least one outer third so product UI can coexist with the crop. Preserve architectural lines and realistic scale. People, if present, should feel incidental and unposed.

## P02 — Everyday Value

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/id/everyday-value/id-everyday-value-v01.webp`

Show an everyday Indonesian city spending moment: coffee, simple rice/noodle meal or café/snack purchase in a contemporary Jakarta setting. Preserve real tray/tableware and local material texture while maintaining a premium editorial composition.

Frame at table/counter/hand level rather than as a product advertisement. Do not print an invented price as a focal element. Any signage should be incidental and plausible, not AI-generated gibberish dominating the scene.

## P03 — Payment Culture

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/id/payment-culture/id-payment-culture-v01.webp`

Show a generic QR, phone or card payment at a contemporary Indonesian café, food counter or small urban retailer. The code/device should be plausible, brand-neutral and secondary to the real human context.

Show the transaction naturally inside a real-feeling setting. Keep hands anatomically correct, device geometry plausible and the payment terminal/phone secondary to the human context. No visible bank/app logos unless they are incidental and accurate; preferably use neutral interfaces.

## P04 — Local Detail

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/id/local-detail/id-local-detail-v01.webp`

Create a refined Jakarta detail with tropical greenery/shadow, concrete/tile, coffee cup or meal utensil, QR stand/receipt edge and warm humid light. Keep it urban and selective, not Bali-themed.

Treat this as a premium editorial insert: tactile materials, quiet composition, a small number of meaningful local cues and excellent light. No souvenir arrangement, flag palette, postcard montage or overloaded prop styling.

## P05 — Desktop Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/id/desktop/id-interface-desktop-v01.webp`

Create a 16:10 premium desktop design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on Indonesia. Use the sample conversion **100 EUR → IDR**. Use warm ivory, charcoal, deep tropical green and muted terracotta/brass. IDR should be the large visual anchor, with one sophisticated Jakarta crop. The screen should communicate: source amount → destination amount → local context. Make the numeric result the largest element. Use only minimal readable text: “Cultural Currency”, country name, currency codes, amount and result. Integrate the P01 visual language as one restrained destination image area. Do not turn the interface into a travel booking site or analytics dashboard.

## P06 — Mobile Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/id/mobile/id-interface-mobile-v01.webp`

Create a 9:16 premium mobile design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on Indonesia, using **100 EUR → IDR**. Translate the desktop hierarchy into a believable single-column mobile flow: compact source/destination selector, large conversion result, one editorial country image, then concise local-value/payment context. Use a compact tropical-urban image beneath a very large IDR result; keep controls quiet and avoid vacation aesthetics. Keep tap targets believable, spacing generous and visible copy minimal. No tiny dashboard cards, fake phone chrome, floating glass panels or crowded widgets.
