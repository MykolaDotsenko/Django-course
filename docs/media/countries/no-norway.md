# Norway (NO) — Country Media Prompt Pack

Currency: `NOK`

Read [Country Media Production System](../00_COUNTRY_MEDIA_SYSTEM.md) before using these prompts. Every generation is assembled as:

`Global Premium Image Contract + role contract + country prompt body below`.

## Visual thesis

Norway should feel contemporary and composed, with Oslo urban design, waterfront/public-space quality and cool natural light rather than fjord-tourism spectacle.

### Preferred local cues

- Oslo waterfront, contemporary architecture or tram/street context
- stone, wood, glass, muted textiles and Nordic café detail
- cold clear light or soft overcast
- coffee/bakery or simple lunch context
- card/contactless payment in normal urban life

### Avoid

- fjord cruise imagery as the only Norway
- Vikings, trolls or flag-heavy scenes
- snow-covered cabin cliché
- extreme wilderness disconnected from everyday spending context

## P01 — Hero Wide

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/no/hero/no-hero-v01.webp`

Photograph Oslo from pedestrian height in soft Nordic daylight: refined contemporary architecture, waterfront/public-space texture or tram movement, a few naturally dressed locals and calm cool air. Use grey-blue, stone, timber, deep green and warm interior light; reserve negative space for UI.

Composition: one coherent contemporary scene, not a collage. Keep the main visual interest away from at least one outer third so product UI can coexist with the crop. Preserve architectural lines and realistic scale. People, if present, should feel incidental and unposed.

## P02 — Everyday Value

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/no/everyday-value/no-everyday-value-v01.webp`

Show a premium but ordinary Oslo café or bakery moment: coffee with a simple pastry/open sandwich or light lunch on natural timber/stone. Keep the atmosphere practical and warm against cool outdoor light, with no visible invented price.

Frame at table/counter/hand level rather than as a product advertisement. Do not print an invented price as a focal element. Any signage should be incidental and plausible, not AI-generated gibberish dominating the scene.

## P03 — Payment Culture

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/no/payment-culture/no-payment-culture-v01.webp`

Show a subtle contactless/card payment at a modern Oslo café, bakery or small grocery/retail counter. Make the interaction ordinary and unposed, with Scandinavian materials and a soft city/waterfront cue behind.

Show the transaction naturally inside a real-feeling setting. Keep hands anatomically correct, device geometry plausible and the payment terminal/phone secondary to the human context. No visible bank/app logos unless they are incidental and accurate; preferably use neutral interfaces.

## P04 — Local Detail

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/no/local-detail/no-local-detail-v01.webp`

Create an Oslo detail study with stone, light timber, tram/waterfront geometry, ceramic cup and cool daylight. The result should feel calm and tactile, not like a nature brochure.

Treat this as a premium editorial insert: tactile materials, quiet composition, a small number of meaningful local cues and excellent light. No souvenir arrangement, flag palette, postcard montage or overloaded prop styling.

## P05 — Desktop Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/no/desktop/no-interface-desktop-v01.webp`

Create a 16:10 premium desktop design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on Norway. Use the sample conversion **100 EUR → NOK**. Use warm ivory against cool stone/blue-grey details, deep forest accent and restrained brass. Make NOK numerals the visual anchor and use one quiet Oslo image. The screen should communicate: source amount → destination amount → local context. Make the numeric result the largest element. Use only minimal readable text: “Cultural Currency”, country name, currency codes, amount and result. Integrate the P01 visual language as one restrained destination image area. Do not turn the interface into a travel booking site or analytics dashboard.

## P06 — Mobile Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/no/mobile/no-interface-mobile-v01.webp`

Create a 9:16 premium mobile design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on Norway, using **100 EUR → NOK**. Translate the desktop hierarchy into a believable single-column mobile flow: compact source/destination selector, large conversion result, one editorial country image, then concise local-value/payment context. Keep the mobile interface sparse and calm, with a large NOK result and one crisp cool-toned Oslo crop. Keep tap targets believable, spacing generous and visible copy minimal. No tiny dashboard cards, fake phone chrome, floating glass panels or crowded widgets.
