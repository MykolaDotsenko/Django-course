# Singapore (SG) — Country Media Prompt Pack

Currency: `SGD`

`Global Premium Image Contract + role contract + country prompt body below`.

## Visual thesis

Singapore should feel precise, dense and effortless: tropical modernity, excellent public infrastructure, hawker/café life and polished architecture balanced without skyline-tourism excess.

### Preferred local cues

- MRT/covered walkways, contemporary towers and shophouse texture used selectively
- hawker-centre, café or compact everyday food context
- tropical greenery integrated with stone, steel and glass
- clean evening light, humid reflections and shaded urban spaces
- card/contactless/QR payment in modern everyday settings

### Avoid

- Marina Bay Sands as mandatory centerpiece
- orchid/merlion tourism shorthand
- sterile futuristic smart-city imagery with no daily life
- excessive neon or luxury-mall aesthetics

## P01 — Hero Wide

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/sg/hero/sg-hero-v01.webp`

Photograph a contemporary Singapore street or transit-linked public space in soft late-afternoon or early-evening light: layered modern architecture, shaded walkways, tropical greenery, clean urban depth and naturally moving residents. Use warm stone, deep green, charcoal and restrained humid reflections. Keep one outer third calm for UI.

Composition: one coherent contemporary scene, not a collage. Keep the main visual interest away from at least one outer third so product UI can coexist with the crop. Preserve architectural lines and realistic scale. People, if present, should feel incidental and unposed.

## P02 — Everyday Value

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/sg/everyday-value/sg-everyday-value-v01.webp`

Show a refined but authentic everyday Singapore spending moment: hawker-centre meal, kopi/coffee, simple café purchase or compact lunch setting. Preserve real table/counter texture and human scale without turning the scene into a food campaign or luxury restaurant.

Frame at table/counter/hand level rather than as a product advertisement. Do not print an invented price as a focal element. Any signage should be incidental and plausible, not AI-generated gibberish dominating the scene.

## P03 — Payment Culture

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/sg/payment-culture/sg-payment-culture-v01.webp`

Show a believable contactless card/phone or generic QR payment in a modern Singapore café, hawker or retail environment. The QR/code interface should be plausible but brand-neutral; the scene should communicate frictionless everyday payment without looking like a fintech advertisement.

Show the transaction naturally inside a real-feeling setting. Keep hands anatomically correct, device geometry plausible and the payment terminal/phone secondary to the human context. No visible bank/app logos unless they are incidental and accurate; preferably use neutral interfaces.

## P04 — Local Detail

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/sg/local-detail/sg-local-detail-v01.webp`

Create a premium local detail using transit geometry, tropical greenery, stainless/stone surfaces, cup/tray or food-counter elements and soft city reflections. Keep the composition architectural and quiet, not a tourist montage.

Treat this as a premium editorial insert: tactile materials, quiet composition, a small number of meaningful local cues and excellent light. No souvenir arrangement, flag palette, postcard montage or overloaded prop styling.

## P05 — Desktop Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/sg/desktop/sg-interface-desktop-v01.webp`

Create a 16:10 premium desktop design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on Singapore. Use the sample conversion **100 EUR → SGD**. Use warm ivory, charcoal, deep tropical green and restrained metallic accents. Make SGD the numeric anchor, with one polished Singapore image and crisp spacing that feels efficient but not sterile. The screen should communicate: source amount → destination amount → local context. Make the numeric result the largest element. Use only minimal readable text: “Cultural Currency”, country name, currency codes, amount and result. Integrate the P01 visual language as one restrained destination image area. Do not turn the interface into a travel booking site or analytics dashboard.

## P06 — Mobile Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/sg/mobile/sg-interface-mobile-v01.webp`

Create a 9:16 premium mobile design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on Singapore, using **100 EUR → SGD**. Translate the desktop hierarchy into a believable single-column mobile flow: compact source/destination selector, large conversion result, one editorial country image, then concise local-value/payment context. Keep the mobile layout highly legible and efficient: large SGD result, one compact Singapore crop, one concise local-context block and almost no decorative UI. Keep tap targets believable, spacing generous and visible copy minimal. No tiny dashboard cards, fake phone chrome, floating glass panels or crowded widgets.
