# Portugal (PT) — Country Media Prompt Pack

Currency: `EUR`

Read [Country Media Production System](../00_COUNTRY_MEDIA_SYSTEM.md) before using these prompts. Every generation is assembled as:

`Global Premium Image Contract + role contract + country prompt body below`.

## Visual thesis

Lisbon texture with restraint: pale stone, tiled façades, hills, trams and Atlantic light can signal Portugal, but the image should remain contemporary and design-led rather than nostalgic tourism.

### Preferred local cues

- Lisbon streets, hills, tram wires or tram used as subtle context
- azulejo/tile texture used sparingly
- espresso/bica, pastel de nata or bakery context
- Atlantic light, pale stone, warm yellow/blue only as small accents
- modern card/contactless payment in everyday retail

### Avoid

- yellow tram as full-frame postcard cliché
- wall-to-wall decorative tiles
- Porto/Lisbon landmark checklist
- nautical explorer imagery

## P01 — Hero Wide

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/pt/hero/pt-hero-v01.webp`

Photograph a refined Lisbon street in soft Atlantic morning or late-afternoon light: pale stone, subtle tile texture, tram wires or a tram small in the depth, hillside layering and naturally moving residents. Use warm off-white, faded blue, deep green and charcoal; leave a quieter outer third for UI.

Composition: one coherent contemporary scene, not a collage. Keep the main visual interest away from at least one outer third so product UI can coexist with the crop. Preserve architectural lines and realistic scale. People, if present, should feel incidental and unposed.

## P02 — Everyday Value

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/pt/everyday-value/pt-everyday-value-v01.webp`

Create an intimate Lisbon café/bakery moment with a bica/espresso and one pastel de nata or simple pastry on a small stone/timber table. Include natural paper/ceramic details and soft street/window context; avoid turning it into a stylized food advertisement.

Frame at table/counter/hand level rather than as a product advertisement. Do not print an invented price as a focal element. Any signage should be incidental and plausible, not AI-generated gibberish dominating the scene.

## P03 — Payment Culture

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/pt/payment-culture/pt-payment-culture-v01.webp`

Show a subtle contactless/card payment at a Lisbon café or bakery counter with restrained tile/stone cues and warm natural light. Terminal and hands must be plausible; no visible bank branding.

Show the transaction naturally inside a real-feeling setting. Keep hands anatomically correct, device geometry plausible and the payment terminal/phone secondary to the human context. No visible bank/app logos unless they are incidental and accurate; preferably use neutral interfaces.

## P04 — Local Detail

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/pt/local-detail/pt-local-detail-v01.webp`

Create a quiet Portuguese detail using one strip of authentic-looking azulejo pattern, pale stone, ceramic espresso cup, bakery paper and reflected Atlantic street light. Keep the pattern secondary and premium, not souvenir-like.

Treat this as a premium editorial insert: tactile materials, quiet composition, a small number of meaningful local cues and excellent light. No souvenir arrangement, flag palette, postcard montage or overloaded prop styling.

## P05 — Desktop Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/pt/desktop/pt-interface-desktop-v01.webp`

Create a 16:10 premium desktop design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on Portugal. Use the sample conversion **100 GBP → EUR**. Use warm limestone, charcoal, deep forest and a faded blue/brass accent. The EUR result should remain dominant, with one restrained Lisbon photograph bringing texture. The screen should communicate: source amount → destination amount → local context. Make the numeric result the largest element. Use only minimal readable text: “Cultural Currency”, country name, currency codes, amount and result. Integrate the P01 visual language as one restrained destination image area. Do not turn the interface into a travel booking site or analytics dashboard.

## P06 — Mobile Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/pt/mobile/pt-interface-mobile-v01.webp`

Create a 9:16 premium mobile design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on Portugal, using **100 GBP → EUR**. Translate the desktop hierarchy into a believable single-column mobile flow: compact source/destination selector, large conversion result, one editorial country image, then concise local-value/payment context. Use a narrow Lisbon street or café crop with strong negative space and an oversized EUR result; avoid decorative tile patterns behind text. Keep tap targets believable, spacing generous and visible copy minimal. No tiny dashboard cards, fake phone chrome, floating glass panels or crowded widgets.
