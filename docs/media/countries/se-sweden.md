## Visual thesis

Stockholm should feel warm-minimal, digitally fluent and human: water, Nordic architecture, transit, café culture and soft natural light with less sterility than generic Scandinavian design imagery.

### Premium material palette

- light timber
- pale stone
- muted textile
- soft water reflections

### Premium mood keywords

- warm Nordic softness
- digital ease
- quiet modernity
- human minimalism

### Premium image goals

- premium first, authentic second, iconic only third;
- communicate the country through atmosphere, material, light, rhythm and everyday behaviour before relying on landmarks;
- make every image feel editorial and useful enough to sit inside an expensive real product, not a tourism campaign;
- preserve one clear visual story per frame and enough compositional calm for interface use where relevant.

### Preferred local cues

- Stockholm waterfront/urban islands or clean street geometry
- subway/tram/ferry cues used naturally
- fika coffee/bakery context
- light timber, stone, muted textiles and brushed metal
- card/mobile payment as ordinary everyday interaction

### Avoid

- IKEA-like showroom shorthand
- blue-yellow flag palette
- Vikings, red cottages or wilderness as default
- empty hyper-minimal scenes with no local life

## P03 — Payment Culture

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/se/payment-culture/se-payment-culture-v01.webp`

**Premium execution:** The payment gesture should occupy only about 20–30% of the visual story; the environment should communicate the country. Use a natural 40–60 mm documentary perspective, anatomically correct hands, plausible device proportions and light timber / pale stone as environmental anchors. Make the moment routine, discreet and trustworthy rather than fintech advertising.

Show a routine card or mobile payment in a contemporary Stockholm café, bakery or small retail environment. Keep the interaction subtle and technologically normal, with no bank/app branding and no implication of universal payment behavior.

Show the transaction naturally inside a real-feeling setting. Keep hands anatomically correct, device geometry plausible and the payment terminal/phone secondary to the human context. No visible bank/app logos unless they are incidental and accurate; preferably use neutral interfaces.
