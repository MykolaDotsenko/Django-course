# South Korea (KR) — Country Media Prompt Pack

Currency: `KRW`

Read [Country Media Production System](../00_COUNTRY_MEDIA_SYSTEM.md) before using these prompts. Every generation is assembled as:

`Global Premium Image Contract + role contract + country prompt body below`.

## Visual thesis

Seoul should feel contemporary, design-forward and digitally fluent: layered urban streets, transit, café/retail life and controlled evening light without K-pop or neon-cyberpunk cliché.

### Preferred local cues

- Seoul streets, subway entrances or layered mid-rise city fabric
- contemporary cafés, convenience or casual food contexts
- stone, glass, brushed metal, timber and subtle signage
- controlled evening light with restrained red/blue accents
- card/mobile payment in everyday urban settings

### Avoid

- K-pop idol imagery
- full cyberpunk neon aesthetic
- traditional palace/hanbok as default country identity
- fake Korean text as dominant decoration

## P01 — Hero Wide

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/kr/hero/kr-hero-v01.webp`

Photograph a sophisticated Seoul urban street at dusk: layered façades, subway/retail cues, warm interior light, subtle reflected color and unposed commuters. Keep saturation controlled and use charcoal, stone, warm paper, deep green and a very restrained red/cobalt accent. Reserve one side for UI.

Composition: one coherent contemporary scene, not a collage. Keep the main visual interest away from at least one outer third so product UI can coexist with the crop. Preserve architectural lines and realistic scale. People, if present, should feel incidental and unposed.

## P02 — Everyday Value

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/kr/everyday-value/kr-everyday-value-v01.webp`

Show a refined Seoul everyday spending moment: café drink with pastry, convenience-style meal, simple lunch tray or compact food-counter context. Make it polished but clearly ordinary, with real materials and natural human presence rather than product styling.

Frame at table/counter/hand level rather than as a product advertisement. Do not print an invented price as a focal element. Any signage should be incidental and plausible, not AI-generated gibberish dominating the scene.

## P03 — Payment Culture

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/kr/payment-culture/kr-payment-culture-v01.webp`

Show a routine card or phone payment at a contemporary Seoul café, convenience-style retailer or food counter. The transaction should feel technologically normal, with generic device UI and no visible app branding.

Show the transaction naturally inside a real-feeling setting. Keep hands anatomically correct, device geometry plausible and the payment terminal/phone secondary to the human context. No visible bank/app logos unless they are incidental and accurate; preferably use neutral interfaces.

## P04 — Local Detail

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/kr/local-detail/kr-local-detail-v01.webp`

Create a restrained Seoul detail combining transit/street geometry, brushed metal, ceramic or paper cup, receipt edge and softly blurred urban signage. Keep text secondary and plausible; avoid cyberpunk color overload.

Treat this as a premium editorial insert: tactile materials, quiet composition, a small number of meaningful local cues and excellent light. No souvenir arrangement, flag palette, postcard montage or overloaded prop styling.

## P05 — Desktop Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/kr/desktop/kr-interface-desktop-v01.webp`

Create a 16:10 premium desktop design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on South Korea. Use the sample conversion **100 EUR → KRW**. Use warm ivory, charcoal, deep forest and a muted cobalt/red accent. KRW numerals should be large and calm, with one sophisticated Seoul crop providing energy without visual noise. The screen should communicate: source amount → destination amount → local context. Make the numeric result the largest element. Use only minimal readable text: “Cultural Currency”, country name, currency codes, amount and result. Integrate the P01 visual language as one restrained destination image area. Do not turn the interface into a travel booking site or analytics dashboard.

## P06 — Mobile Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/kr/mobile/kr-interface-mobile-v01.webp`

Create a 9:16 premium mobile design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on South Korea, using **100 EUR → KRW**. Translate the desktop hierarchy into a believable single-column mobile flow: compact source/destination selector, large conversion result, one editorial country image, then concise local-value/payment context. Use a tall Seoul street crop with controlled evening light under or beside the large KRW result; keep the UI quiet and precise. Keep tap targets believable, spacing generous and visible copy minimal. No tiny dashboard cards, fake phone chrome, floating glass panels or crowded widgets.
