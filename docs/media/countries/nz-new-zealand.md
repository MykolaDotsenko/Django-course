`Global Premium Image Contract + role contract + country prompt body below`.

## Visual thesis

New Zealand should feel small-scale, thoughtful and contemporary: Auckland or Wellington urban texture, café culture, waterfront light and understated natural connection without landscape-tourism dominance.

### Premium material palette

- timber
- cool stone
- maritime glass/reflection
- matte metal

### Premium mood keywords

- compact calm
- maritime softness
- thoughtful design
- understated modernity

### Premium image goals

- premium first, authentic second, iconic only third;
- communicate the country through atmosphere, material, light, rhythm and everyday behaviour before relying on landmarks;
- make every image feel editorial and useful enough to sit inside an expensive real product, not a tourism campaign;
- preserve one clear visual story per frame and enough compositional calm for interface use where relevant.

### Preferred local cues

- Auckland/Wellington waterfront or compact urban streets
- coffee/café culture and local everyday retail
- timber, stone, metal and cool maritime daylight
- ferry/bus/city infrastructure used subtly
- card/contactless payment in daily settings

### Avoid

- mountain/lake postcard as the only identity
- sheep, hobbit or adventure-tourism clichés
- silver fern/flag branding
- empty nature with no connection to the product's everyday-money story

## P03 — Payment Culture

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/nz/payment-culture/nz-payment-culture-v01.webp`

**Premium execution:** The payment gesture should occupy only about 20–30% of the visual story; the environment should communicate the country. Use a natural 40–60 mm documentary perspective, anatomically correct hands, plausible device proportions and timber / cool stone as environmental anchors. Make the moment routine, discreet and trustworthy rather than fintech advertising.

Show a routine card/contactless payment at an Auckland/Wellington café or neighborhood retailer. Keep the exchange natural, terminal generic and local identity conveyed through environment rather than symbols.

Show the transaction naturally inside a real-feeling setting. Keep hands anatomically correct, device geometry plausible and the payment terminal/phone secondary to the human context. No visible bank/app logos unless they are incidental and accurate; preferably use neutral interfaces.
