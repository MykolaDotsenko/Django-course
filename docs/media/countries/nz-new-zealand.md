# New Zealand (NZ) — Country Media Prompt Pack

Currency: `NZD`

Read [Country Media Production System](../00_COUNTRY_MEDIA_SYSTEM.md) before using these prompts. Every generation is assembled as:

`Global Premium Image Contract + role contract + country prompt body below`.

## Visual thesis

New Zealand should feel small-scale, thoughtful and contemporary: Auckland or Wellington urban texture, café culture, waterfront light and understated natural connection without landscape-tourism dominance.

### Preferred local cues

- Auckland/Wellington waterfront or compact urban streets
- coffee/café culture and local everyday retail
- timber, stone, metal and cool maritime daylight
- ferry/bus/city infrastructure used subtly
- card/contactless payment in daily settings

### Avoid

- mountain/lake postcard as the only identity
- sheep, hobbit or adventure-tourism clichés
- silver fern/flag branding
- empty nature with no connection to the product's everyday-money story

## P01 — Hero Wide

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/nz/hero/nz-hero-v01.webp`

Photograph Wellington or Auckland in soft maritime daylight: compact contemporary city architecture, waterfront/ferry or street movement, a few unposed locals and clean atmospheric depth. Use stone, deep green, blue-grey and warm interior accents; preserve negative space for UI.

Composition: one coherent contemporary scene, not a collage. Keep the main visual interest away from at least one outer third so product UI can coexist with the crop. Preserve architectural lines and realistic scale. People, if present, should feel incidental and unposed.

## P02 — Everyday Value

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/nz/everyday-value/nz-everyday-value-v01.webp`

Show a believable New Zealand café/bakery purchase: coffee with pastry/simple lunch item on natural timber/stone, with soft coastal-city light and relaxed but refined composition. Avoid rustic tourism styling.

Frame at table/counter/hand level rather than as a product advertisement. Do not print an invented price as a focal element. Any signage should be incidental and plausible, not AI-generated gibberish dominating the scene.

## P03 — Payment Culture

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/nz/payment-culture/nz-payment-culture-v01.webp`

Show a routine card/contactless payment at an Auckland/Wellington café or neighborhood retailer. Keep the exchange natural, terminal generic and local identity conveyed through environment rather than symbols.

Show the transaction naturally inside a real-feeling setting. Keep hands anatomically correct, device geometry plausible and the payment terminal/phone secondary to the human context. No visible bank/app logos unless they are incidental and accurate; preferably use neutral interfaces.

## P04 — Local Detail

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/nz/local-detail/nz-local-detail-v01.webp`

Create a quiet local detail with waterfront reflection, timber, ceramic coffee cup, transit/ferry texture and paper receipt edge. Keep it materially simple and premium.

Treat this as a premium editorial insert: tactile materials, quiet composition, a small number of meaningful local cues and excellent light. No souvenir arrangement, flag palette, postcard montage or overloaded prop styling.

## P05 — Desktop Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/nz/desktop/nz-interface-desktop-v01.webp`

Create a 16:10 premium desktop design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on New Zealand. Use the sample conversion **100 EUR → NZD**. Use warm ivory, charcoal, deep fern/forest green and cool maritime grey. Make NZD the numeric hero and use one compact waterfront/city image. The screen should communicate: source amount → destination amount → local context. Make the numeric result the largest element. Use only minimal readable text: “Cultural Currency”, country name, currency codes, amount and result. Integrate the P01 visual language as one restrained destination image area. Do not turn the interface into a travel booking site or analytics dashboard.

## P06 — Mobile Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/nz/mobile/nz-interface-mobile-v01.webp`

Create a 9:16 premium mobile design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on New Zealand, using **100 EUR → NZD**. Translate the desktop hierarchy into a believable single-column mobile flow: compact source/destination selector, large conversion result, one editorial country image, then concise local-value/payment context. Keep the mobile concept light and calm, with one waterfront/café crop and very strong NZD hierarchy. Keep tap targets believable, spacing generous and visible copy minimal. No tiny dashboard cards, fake phone chrome, floating glass panels or crowded widgets.
