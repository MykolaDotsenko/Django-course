# Australia (AU) — Country Media Prompt Pack

Currency: `AUD`

Read [Country Media Production System](../00_COUNTRY_MEDIA_SYSTEM.md) before using these prompts. Every generation is assembled as:

`Global Premium Image Contract + role contract + country prompt body below`.

## Visual thesis

Australia should feel bright, contemporary and design-aware through Sydney or Melbourne urban life, café culture and strong natural light—not beaches, wildlife or tourism shorthand.

### Preferred local cues

- Melbourne laneways/architecture or Sydney contemporary streets/harbour context
- high-quality café/brunch culture used naturally
- stone, concrete, timber and strong daylight
- tram/transit cues especially for Melbourne
- tap/card payment in everyday venues

### Avoid

- kangaroos, koalas or surf imagery as default
- Sydney Opera House as mandatory hero
- sunset beach tourism
- overly casual lifestyle-ad aesthetic

## P01 — Hero Wide

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/au/hero/au-hero-v01.webp`

Photograph Melbourne or Sydney in crisp but softened natural light: sophisticated street/laneway or harbour-edge architecture, tram/transit or pedestrian movement, café/retail depth and unposed city life. Use warm stone, eucalyptus/deep green, charcoal and clear sky/water accents sparingly. Keep a quiet third for UI.

Composition: one coherent contemporary scene, not a collage. Keep the main visual interest away from at least one outer third so product UI can coexist with the crop. Preserve architectural lines and realistic scale. People, if present, should feel incidental and unposed.

## P02 — Everyday Value

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/au/everyday-value/au-everyday-value-v01.webp`

Show an elevated but ordinary Australian café moment: flat white, pastry or simple brunch element on a stone/timber table, natural daylight and subtle city context. Make it look genuinely frequented rather than a styled influencer shoot.

Frame at table/counter/hand level rather than as a product advertisement. Do not print an invented price as a focal element. Any signage should be incidental and plausible, not AI-generated gibberish dominating the scene.

## P03 — Payment Culture

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/au/payment-culture/au-payment-culture-v01.webp`

Show a normal tap-to-pay card/phone transaction in a Melbourne/Sydney café, bakery or small urban retailer. Keep the terminal neutral, hands correct and environment design-led but not luxurious.

Show the transaction naturally inside a real-feeling setting. Keep hands anatomically correct, device geometry plausible and the payment terminal/phone secondary to the human context. No visible bank/app logos unless they are incidental and accurate; preferably use neutral interfaces.

## P04 — Local Detail

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/au/local-detail/au-local-detail-v01.webp`

Create a refined Australian urban detail with coffee cup, tram/harbour geometry, concrete/timber, paper receipt edge and sharp natural light softened by shadow. No beach or wildlife props.

Treat this as a premium editorial insert: tactile materials, quiet composition, a small number of meaningful local cues and excellent light. No souvenir arrangement, flag palette, postcard montage or overloaded prop styling.

## P05 — Desktop Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/au/desktop/au-interface-desktop-v01.webp`

Create a 16:10 premium desktop design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on Australia. Use the sample conversion **100 EUR → AUD**. Use warm ivory, charcoal, deep eucalyptus green and muted sun-warmed brass. Make AUD numerals dominant and integrate one bright but restrained city photograph. The screen should communicate: source amount → destination amount → local context. Make the numeric result the largest element. Use only minimal readable text: “Cultural Currency”, country name, currency codes, amount and result. Integrate the P01 visual language as one restrained destination image area. Do not turn the interface into a travel booking site or analytics dashboard.

## P06 — Mobile Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/au/mobile/au-interface-mobile-v01.webp`

Create a 9:16 premium mobile design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on Australia, using **100 EUR → AUD**. Translate the desktop hierarchy into a believable single-column mobile flow: compact source/destination selector, large conversion result, one editorial country image, then concise local-value/payment context. Use one crisp café/city crop with strong daylight and an oversized AUD result; avoid beach imagery in the core mobile screen. Keep tap targets believable, spacing generous and visible copy minimal. No tiny dashboard cards, fake phone chrome, floating glass panels or crowded widgets.
