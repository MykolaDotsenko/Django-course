# Finland (FI) — Country Media Prompt Pack

Currency: `EUR`

Read [Country Media Production System](../00_COUNTRY_MEDIA_SYSTEM.md) before using these prompts. Every generation is assembled as:

`Global Premium Image Contract + role contract + country prompt body below`.

## Visual thesis

Quiet Nordic confidence: Helsinki should feel precise, calm, tactile and human rather than sterile. The visual identity comes from granite, pale stone, timber, tram infrastructure, Baltic light, restrained interiors and ordinary city life.

### Preferred local cues

- Helsinki streets, tram wires or a contemporary tram used subtly
- granite, pale plaster, brushed metal, light timber and muted textiles
- Nordic café culture, rye bread or korvapuusti as secondary details
- harbour/Baltic light, overcast clarity or long soft evening light
- modern Finnish design language without turning the scene into a furniture catalogue

### Avoid

- aurora, reindeer, Santa or Lapland as default Finland shorthand
- blue-and-white flag domination
- snowy wilderness unless the prompt specifically calls for it
- generic Scandinavian cabin aesthetic with no urban Finland specificity

## P01 — Hero Wide

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/fi/hero/fi-hero-v01.webp`

Photograph Helsinki at blue hour or a soft overcast late afternoon from pedestrian height: refined stone façades, a contemporary tram entering or leaving the frame, subtle Baltic humidity in the light, clean but lived-in pavement and a few naturally dressed commuters. Use a 35–50 mm documentary/editorial perspective, soft depth rather than extreme bokeh, muted stone/forest/brass tones, and reserve calmer negative space along one side.

Composition: one coherent contemporary scene, not a collage. Keep the main visual interest away from at least one outer third so product UI can coexist with the crop. Preserve architectural lines and realistic scale. People, if present, should feel incidental and unposed.

## P02 — Everyday Value

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/fi/everyday-value/fi-everyday-value-v01.webp`

Build an intimate Helsinki café moment around a ceramic coffee cup, a fresh korvapuusti or small bakery item, a simple tray/plate and high-quality Nordic materials such as pale timber, linen or stone. Let a coat sleeve, hand or window reflection imply a real person without staging a lifestyle ad. The image should communicate an ordinary €10–€20 type of everyday moment without showing a fabricated numeric price.

Frame at table/counter/hand level rather than as a product advertisement. Do not print an invented price as a focal element. Any signage should be incidental and plausible, not AI-generated gibberish dominating the scene.

## P03 — Payment Culture

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/fi/payment-culture/fi-payment-culture-v01.webp`

Show a normal contactless card or phone payment in a contemporary Helsinki café, bakery or small design shop. Include subtle Finnish environmental cues through materials, window light or tram/street context beyond the counter rather than flags. The cashier/customer interaction should feel efficient and ordinary, with the terminal readable as a terminal but not brand-prominent.

Show the transaction naturally inside a real-feeling setting. Keep hands anatomically correct, device geometry plausible and the payment terminal/phone secondary to the human context. No visible bank/app logos unless they are incidental and accurate; preferably use neutral interfaces.

## P04 — Local Detail

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/fi/local-detail/fi-local-detail-v01.webp`

Create a quiet Helsinki detail study combining two or three credible cues: tram stop geometry or ticketing texture, wet granite, a folded café receipt with no readable invented totals, ceramic cup, brushed metal and a glimpse of waterfront or street beyond. The image should feel like an editorial interlude about everyday urban Finland.

Treat this as a premium editorial insert: tactile materials, quiet composition, a small number of meaningful local cues and excellent light. No souvenir arrangement, flag palette, postcard montage or overloaded prop styling.

## P05 — Desktop Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/fi/desktop/fi-interface-desktop-v01.webp`

Create a 16:10 premium desktop design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on Finland. Use the sample conversion **100 USD → EUR**. Use an ivory/stone canvas, deep forest green, charcoal text and a very restrained warm brass accent. Finnish context should enter through a subtle Helsinki image and disciplined Nordic spacing, not through flag colors. The screen should communicate: source amount → destination amount → local context. Make the numeric result the largest element. Use only minimal readable text: “Cultural Currency”, country name, currency codes, amount and result. Integrate the P01 visual language as one restrained destination image area. Do not turn the interface into a travel booking site or analytics dashboard.

## P06 — Mobile Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/fi/mobile/fi-interface-mobile-v01.webp`

Create a 9:16 premium mobile design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on Finland, using **100 USD → EUR**. Translate the desktop hierarchy into a believable single-column mobile flow: compact source/destination selector, large conversion result, one editorial country image, then concise local-value/payment context. Make the mobile version especially calm and Nordic: one strong number, almost no decoration, one Helsinki crop and a single understated local-context section. Keep tap targets believable, spacing generous and visible copy minimal. No tiny dashboard cards, fake phone chrome, floating glass panels or crowded widgets.
