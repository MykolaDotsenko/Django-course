# South Africa (ZA) — Country Media Prompt Pack

Currency: `ZAR`

Read [Country Media Production System](../00_COUNTRY_MEDIA_SYSTEM.md) before using these prompts. Every generation is assembled as:

`Global Premium Image Contract + role contract + country prompt body below`.

## Visual thesis

South Africa should feel contemporary, diverse and place-specific through Cape Town or Johannesburg urban life, café/retail context and distinctive light without safari or landscape-tourism shorthand.

### Preferred local cues

- Cape Town/Johannesburg contemporary streets and architecture
- mountain/landscape presence only when naturally integrated into city context
- coffee, bakery, simple meal or market/café everyday spending
- stone, concrete, timber, metal and strong natural light
- card/contactless payment in modern daily commerce

### Avoid

- safari animals as default South Africa
- flag-color or rainbow-nation visual cliché
- luxury wine estate as the whole country
- township imagery used exploitatively or as aesthetic shorthand

## P01 — Hero Wide

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/za/hero/za-hero-v01.webp`

Photograph Cape Town or Johannesburg in refined late-afternoon light: contemporary urban architecture, street/café/retail life, diverse unposed residents and—if Cape Town—a mountain presence only as a distant compositional layer, not the subject. Use stone, deep green, charcoal and warm sunlit neutrals. Preserve a calm area for UI.

Composition: one coherent contemporary scene, not a collage. Keep the main visual interest away from at least one outer third so product UI can coexist with the crop. Preserve architectural lines and realistic scale. People, if present, should feel incidental and unposed.

## P02 — Everyday Value

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/za/everyday-value/za-everyday-value-v01.webp`

Show a believable South African urban café, bakery or casual meal moment with coffee/simple food, natural materials and real human presence. Keep it contemporary and grounded, avoiding luxury-wine or safari styling.

Frame at table/counter/hand level rather than as a product advertisement. Do not print an invented price as a focal element. Any signage should be incidental and plausible, not AI-generated gibberish dominating the scene.

## P03 — Payment Culture

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/za/payment-culture/za-payment-culture-v01.webp`

Show a routine contactless/card payment at a Cape Town/Johannesburg café, bakery or small retailer. The transaction should feel normal and inclusive, with device branding neutral and local identity carried by environment.

Show the transaction naturally inside a real-feeling setting. Keep hands anatomically correct, device geometry plausible and the payment terminal/phone secondary to the human context. No visible bank/app logos unless they are incidental and accurate; preferably use neutral interfaces.

## P04 — Local Detail

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/za/local-detail/za-local-detail-v01.webp`

Create a tactile South African city detail using stone/concrete, coffee cup or food-counter object, greenery/sun shadow, metal/timber and a subtle city/mountain reflection where appropriate. Avoid wildlife or flag motifs.

Treat this as a premium editorial insert: tactile materials, quiet composition, a small number of meaningful local cues and excellent light. No souvenir arrangement, flag palette, postcard montage or overloaded prop styling.

## P05 — Desktop Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/za/desktop/za-interface-desktop-v01.webp`

Create a 16:10 premium desktop design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on South Africa. Use the sample conversion **100 EUR → ZAR**. Use warm ivory, charcoal, deep forest and sun-warmed bronze. ZAR should be the dominant numeric element, with one refined contemporary South African city image. The screen should communicate: source amount → destination amount → local context. Make the numeric result the largest element. Use only minimal readable text: “Cultural Currency”, country name, currency codes, amount and result. Integrate the P01 visual language as one restrained destination image area. Do not turn the interface into a travel booking site or analytics dashboard.

## P06 — Mobile Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/za/mobile/za-interface-mobile-v01.webp`

Create a 9:16 premium mobile design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on South Africa, using **100 EUR → ZAR**. Translate the desktop hierarchy into a believable single-column mobile flow: compact source/destination selector, large conversion result, one editorial country image, then concise local-value/payment context. Keep the mobile concept clean and sunlit: one urban crop, large ZAR result and concise local context with no travel-brochure imagery. Keep tap targets believable, spacing generous and visible copy minimal. No tiny dashboard cards, fake phone chrome, floating glass panels or crowded widgets.
