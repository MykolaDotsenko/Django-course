## Visual thesis

South Africa should feel contemporary, diverse and place-specific through Cape Town or Johannesburg urban life, café/retail context and distinctive light without safari or landscape-tourism shorthand.

### Premium material palette

- stone and concrete
- warm timber
- brushed metal
- sunlit shadow

### Premium mood keywords

- sunlit urban confidence
- grounded elegance
- contemporary diversity
- strong sense of place

### Premium image goals

- premium first, authentic second, iconic only third;
- communicate the country through atmosphere, material, light, rhythm and everyday behaviour before relying on landmarks;
- make every image feel editorial and useful enough to sit inside an expensive real product, not a tourism campaign;
- preserve one clear visual story per frame and enough compositional calm for interface use where relevant.

### Preferred local cues

- Cape Town/Johannesburg contemporary streets and architecture
- mountain/landscape presence only when naturally integrated into city context
- coffee, bakery, simple meal or market/café everyday spending
- stone, concrete, timber, metal and strong natural light
- card/contactless payment in modern daily commerce

### Avoid

- safari animals as default South Africa
- flag-color or rainbow-nation visual cliché
- luxury wine estate as the whole country
- township imagery used exploitatively or as aesthetic shorthand
