# United Kingdom (GB) — Country Media Prompt Pack

Currency: `GBP`

Read [Country Media Production System](../00_COUNTRY_MEDIA_SYSTEM.md) before using these prompts. Every generation is assembled as:

`Global Premium Image Contract + role contract + country prompt body below`.

## Visual thesis

London sophistication without souvenir Britain: combine heritage materials, contemporary city rhythm, transit and moody natural light in a restrained financial-editorial language.

### Preferred local cues

- London stone/brick streets, contemporary architecture and subtle red transit cues
- black metal, dark green, stone, timber and brushed brass materials
- café/bakery/pub-adjacent everyday context without pub stereotype
- overcast or post-rain light used elegantly
- contactless transit/retail cues without brand logos

### Avoid

- Union Jack domination
- red bus + Big Ben + phone box all in one frame
- royal imagery as shorthand
- Harry Potter or vintage-tourism styling

## P01 — Hero Wide

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/gb/hero/gb-hero-v01.webp`

Photograph a refined London street after a light rain or under soft overcast daylight: layered stone and brick architecture, subtle contemporary transit movement, dark metal railings and a few unposed pedestrians. A red bus may appear only as a small compositional accent, not the subject. Favor charcoal, stone, deep green and warm window light; reserve calm space for UI.

Composition: one coherent contemporary scene, not a collage. Keep the main visual interest away from at least one outer third so product UI can coexist with the crop. Preserve architectural lines and realistic scale. People, if present, should feel incidental and unposed.

## P02 — Everyday Value

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/gb/everyday-value/gb-everyday-value-v01.webp`

Show a premium but ordinary London café/bakery moment: flat white or tea with a small pastry/sandwich on a stone or timber surface, natural paper packaging and a glimpse of urban street texture. Avoid twee afternoon-tea styling or chain branding.

Frame at table/counter/hand level rather than as a product advertisement. Do not print an invented price as a focal element. Any signage should be incidental and plausible, not AI-generated gibberish dominating the scene.

## P03 — Payment Culture

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/gb/payment-culture/gb-payment-culture-v01.webp`

Show a natural contactless card/phone payment at a contemporary London café, bakery or small retail counter. The terminal should be generic; background details can hint at transport/city context. Keep it efficient, discreet and observational.

Show the transaction naturally inside a real-feeling setting. Keep hands anatomically correct, device geometry plausible and the payment terminal/phone secondary to the human context. No visible bank/app logos unless they are incidental and accurate; preferably use neutral interfaces.

## P04 — Local Detail

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/gb/local-detail/gb-local-detail-v01.webp`

Create a tactile London detail: wet pavement reflection, dark metal, stone, a ceramic cup, folded paper receipt without readable totals and a blurred transit/street cue. The image should feel like an international design magazine, not a tourism campaign.

Treat this as a premium editorial insert: tactile materials, quiet composition, a small number of meaningful local cues and excellent light. No souvenir arrangement, flag palette, postcard montage or overloaded prop styling.

## P05 — Desktop Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/gb/desktop/gb-interface-desktop-v01.webp`

Create a 16:10 premium desktop design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on United Kingdom. Use the sample conversion **100 EUR → GBP**. Use warm stone, charcoal, deep forest and restrained brass; make GBP numerals the hero. A moody London image can sit in one destination panel with strong editorial crop. The screen should communicate: source amount → destination amount → local context. Make the numeric result the largest element. Use only minimal readable text: “Cultural Currency”, country name, currency codes, amount and result. Integrate the P01 visual language as one restrained destination image area. Do not turn the interface into a travel booking site or analytics dashboard.

## P06 — Mobile Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/gb/mobile/gb-interface-mobile-v01.webp`

Create a 9:16 premium mobile design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on United Kingdom, using **100 EUR → GBP**. Translate the desktop hierarchy into a believable single-column mobile flow: compact source/destination selector, large conversion result, one editorial country image, then concise local-value/payment context. Favor a compact London street crop with stone/rain texture and a very large GBP result; no decorative Union Jack motifs. Keep tap targets believable, spacing generous and visible copy minimal. No tiny dashboard cards, fake phone chrome, floating glass panels or crowded widgets.
