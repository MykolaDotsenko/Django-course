# Denmark (DK) — Country Media Prompt Pack

Currency: `DKK`

Read [Country Media Production System](../00_COUNTRY_MEDIA_SYSTEM.md) before using these prompts. Every generation is assembled as:

`Global Premium Image Contract + role contract + country prompt body below`.

## Visual thesis

Copenhagen should feel design-literate, warm, social and functional: bicycles, harbour/streets, café culture and beautiful everyday materials without lifestyle-blog cliché.

### Preferred local cues

- Copenhagen cycling infrastructure and urban streets used naturally
- harbour or brick/plaster architecture
- coffee/pastry/bakery context
- oak/timber, stone, ceramic and dark metal
- contactless/card payment in everyday retail

### Avoid

- staged hygge candle tableaux
- Little Mermaid landmark dependence
- red-white flag styling
- designer-furniture showroom look

## P01 — Hero Wide

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/dk/hero/dk-hero-v01.webp`

Photograph Copenhagen in soft morning or late-afternoon light: elegant street/harbour architecture, bicycles integrated as transport rather than props, a few moving locals and warm shop/café interiors. Use muted brick, stone, timber, deep green and soft blue-grey. Leave one outer third relatively calm for UI.

Composition: one coherent contemporary scene, not a collage. Keep the main visual interest away from at least one outer third so product UI can coexist with the crop. Preserve architectural lines and realistic scale. People, if present, should feel incidental and unposed.

## P02 — Everyday Value

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/dk/everyday-value/dk-everyday-value-v01.webp`

Create an authentic Copenhagen bakery/café moment with coffee, a Danish pastry or simple breakfast item, ceramic and warm wood/stone. Let natural crumbs, jacket sleeve or window reflection make it feel lived-in rather than staged hygge.

Frame at table/counter/hand level rather than as a product advertisement. Do not print an invented price as a focal element. Any signage should be incidental and plausible, not AI-generated gibberish dominating the scene.

## P03 — Payment Culture

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/dk/payment-culture/dk-payment-culture-v01.webp`

Show a routine contactless/card payment at a modern Copenhagen café or bakery, with subtle bicycle/street context in the background. Keep the terminal generic and the exchange natural.

Show the transaction naturally inside a real-feeling setting. Keep hands anatomically correct, device geometry plausible and the payment terminal/phone secondary to the human context. No visible bank/app logos unless they are incidental and accurate; preferably use neutral interfaces.

## P04 — Local Detail

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/dk/local-detail/dk-local-detail-v01.webp`

Create a refined Copenhagen detail combining bicycle-frame geometry, warm timber, ceramic cup, brick/harbour reflection and dark metal. Avoid candles, flags and overly curated lifestyle props.

Treat this as a premium editorial insert: tactile materials, quiet composition, a small number of meaningful local cues and excellent light. No souvenir arrangement, flag palette, postcard montage or overloaded prop styling.

## P05 — Desktop Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/dk/desktop/dk-interface-desktop-v01.webp`

Create a 16:10 premium desktop design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on Denmark. Use the sample conversion **100 EUR → DKK**. Use ivory, charcoal, deep forest and warm oak/brass notes. The DKK result should be large and calm; Copenhagen identity comes from one excellent photograph and spacious composition. The screen should communicate: source amount → destination amount → local context. Make the numeric result the largest element. Use only minimal readable text: “Cultural Currency”, country name, currency codes, amount and result. Integrate the P01 visual language as one restrained destination image area. Do not turn the interface into a travel booking site or analytics dashboard.

## P06 — Mobile Interface Concept

**Target:** `docs/assets/country-interface-concepts/dk/mobile/dk-interface-mobile-v01.webp`

Create a 9:16 premium mobile design concept for Cultural Currency Converter focused on Denmark, using **100 EUR → DKK**. Translate the desktop hierarchy into a believable single-column mobile flow: compact source/destination selector, large conversion result, one editorial country image, then concise local-value/payment context. Favor a clean single-column hierarchy with large DKK result, one urban cycling/harbour crop and minimal supporting copy. Keep tap targets believable, spacing generous and visible copy minimal. No tiny dashboard cards, fake phone chrome, floating glass panels or crowded widgets.
