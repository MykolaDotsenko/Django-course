# Canada (CA) — Country Media Prompt Pack

Currency: `CAD`

Read [Country Media Production System](../00_COUNTRY_MEDIA_SYSTEM.md) before using these prompts. Every generation is assembled as:

`Global Premium Image Contract + role contract + country prompt body below`.

## Visual thesis

Canada should feel contemporary, open and urban: Toronto or Vancouver everyday life, transit, café culture and climate-aware material texture without falling into wilderness or maple-leaf clichés.

### Premium material palette

- glass and brick
- cool stone
- warm timber
- wet-street reflections

### Premium mood keywords

- open modernity
- calm cosmopolitan polish
- inclusive urban confidence
- cool clarity

### Premium image goals

- premium first, authentic second, iconic only third;
- communicate the country through atmosphere, material, light, rhythm and everyday behaviour before relying on landmarks;
- make every image feel editorial and useful enough to sit inside an expensive real product, not a tourism campaign;
- preserve one clear visual story per frame and enough compositional calm for interface use where relevant.

### Preferred local cues

- Toronto/Vancouver contemporary streets and transit
- glass, brick, concrete, timber and cool natural light
- coffee/bakery/casual lunch context
- diverse everyday urban population
- card/contactless payment in normal city retail

### Avoid

- maple leaves and flags as dominant motifs
- mountains/lakes as the only Canadian identity
- hockey/lumberjack stereotypes
- generic North American corporate stock photography

**Premium execution:** Use a believable 35–50 mm full-frame-equivalent editorial perspective, eye level or only slightly elevated, with realistic architectural geometry and medium depth rather than extreme bokeh. Grade around glass and brick, cool stone, warm timber and aim for open modernity + calm cosmopolitan polish. Make the environment carry identity; keep roughly 25–35% of the frame visually calm enough to coexist with product UI.

Photograph Toronto or Vancouver at soft morning/late-afternoon light: contemporary architecture, streetcar/transit or pedestrian rhythm, diverse unposed locals and a restrained hint of climate/weather. Use cool stone/glass, brick, deep green and warm interiors. Reserve calmer negative space for UI.

Composition: one coherent contemporary scene, not a collage. Keep the main visual interest away from at least one outer third so product UI can coexist with the crop. Preserve architectural lines and realistic scale. People, if present, should feel incidental and unposed.

## P02 — Everyday Value

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/ca/everyday-value/ca-everyday-value-v01.webp`

**Premium execution:** Treat this as a 50–70 mm observational editorial frame, not food advertising. Let tactile local materials—glass and brick, cool stone, warm timber—carry perceived quality. Use one believable spending ritual, one clear focal point, natural imperfections and restrained depth of field. The result should express open modernity without looking staged or aspirational.

Create a believable Canadian urban café/bakery or casual lunch moment with coffee, pastry/sandwich or simple food, tactile paper/ceramic and city depth. Avoid chain branding and wilderness props.

Frame at table/counter/hand level rather than as a product advertisement. Do not print an invented price as a focal element. Any signage should be incidental and plausible, not AI-generated gibberish dominating the scene.

## P03 — Payment Culture

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/ca/payment-culture/ca-payment-culture-v01.webp`

**Premium execution:** The payment gesture should occupy only about 20–30% of the visual story; the environment should communicate the country. Use a natural 40–60 mm documentary perspective, anatomically correct hands, plausible device proportions and glass and brick / cool stone as environmental anchors. Make the moment routine, discreet and trustworthy rather than fintech advertising.

Show a routine card/contactless payment at a Toronto/Vancouver café, bakery or neighborhood retailer. Keep the terminal generic and the environment modern, warm and socially diverse.

Show the transaction naturally inside a real-feeling setting. Keep hands anatomically correct, device geometry plausible and the payment terminal/phone secondary to the human context. No visible bank/app logos unless they are incidental and accurate; preferably use neutral interfaces.

## P04 — Local Detail

**Target:** `static/images/country-media/ca/local-detail/ca-local-detail-v01.webp`

**Premium execution:** Use a close observational 65–85 mm feel with two to four carefully chosen elements, tactile side-light and real material texture. Build around glass and brick, cool stone, warm timber and inclusive urban confidence. Leave breathing room; avoid decorative flat-lays, souvenir arrangements and perfect AI symmetry.

Create a Canadian city detail with transit geometry, brick/glass reflection, ceramic cup, paper bag/receipt and subtle weather texture such as wet pavement or cool window light. No maple-leaf props.

Treat this as a premium editorial insert: tactile materials, quiet composition, a small number of meaningful local cues and excellent light. No souvenir arrangement, flag palette, postcard montage or overloaded prop styling.
